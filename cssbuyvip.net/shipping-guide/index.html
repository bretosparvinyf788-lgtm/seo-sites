Understand the variables behind parcel cost, route eligibility and delivery expectations.

Design the parcel before submission

International freight depends on destination, actual or volumetric weight, dimensions, route rules, packing choices and optional services. A low item price can become inefficient when the product is bulky or route-sensitive.

Build two scenarios

  • Scenario A preserves retail boxes and prioritizes protection.
  • Scenario B removes unnecessary packaging and compresses soft goods.
  • Compare preferred and backup routes before clicking submit.
  • Record the estimated deposit and the final verified charge.
