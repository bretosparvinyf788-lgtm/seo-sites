CSSBuy QC Guide

Turn warehouse photographs into a structured ship-or-return decision.

Inspect in three passes

First confirm identity: item, variant, color, quantity and size. Second inspect visible condition: damage, stains, hardware, missing parts and packaging. Third compare specifications: measurements, placement, shape and features that matter to you.

Request targeted evidence

Do not ask for “more photos” without a purpose. Describe the location, angle and measurement method so the result can change your decision.

  • Check each item soon after warehouse arrival.
  • Do not confuse warehouse storage with the seller’s return window.
  • Choose packaging services according to the product’s failure risk.
