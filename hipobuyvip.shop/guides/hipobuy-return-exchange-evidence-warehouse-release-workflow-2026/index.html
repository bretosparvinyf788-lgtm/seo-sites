Hipobuy Return Policy 2026: 5-Day Refund and Exchange Guide

Can you refund a Hipobuy order? Often, yes—but the published warehouse return window is short. This guide explains the 5-day guarantee, the 120-hour clock, the stated 5 RMB service fee, seller approval, evidence and safe parcel release.

Official-policy check

Hipobuy's published returns page was reviewed on September 11, 2026. Live eligibility still depends on the specific product, seller response, item condition and current account record.

Create an evidence packet before contacting support

A weak return request says, “This is wrong.” A strong request shows what was ordered, what arrived and why the difference matters. Build a compact evidence packet before opening a case.

Start with the original product link and a screenshot of the selected option. Add the order number, seller or shop name, color, size, model, quantity and any purchase note. Save the warehouse images that show the problem. Mark the relevant area when the defect is small. If the issue is a measurement, include the expected source and a warehouse photo showing the tape from end to end.

For a wrong variant, place the order screenshot beside the warehouse label. For missing accessories, use the listing description identifying the included part and one warehouse image showing the complete contents. For damage, request a close-up and a wider image proving the mark belongs to the received item.

Keep the packet factual. “The left sleeve is 4 cm shorter than the right sleeve” is more useful than “terrible quality.” Specific evidence is easier for an agent to translate and for a seller to answer.

Classify the problem before choosing return or exchange

Not every warehouse exception needs the same remedy. Classify the problem first.

A seller error includes the wrong color, size, model, quantity or missing advertised component. A condition problem includes stains, tears or broken hardware. A specification problem occurs when a measurable feature conflicts with the listing or a confirmed seller answer. A preference problem occurs when the item matches the order but the buyer dislikes the fit, shade or feel.

This distinction matters because responsibility, domestic return cost and remedies may differ. An exchange is sensible when the correct variant is in stock, the item is replaceable and the turnaround fits the parcel plan. A return and fresh order may be cleaner when stock is uncertain, the listing changed or another seller offers a better-documented option.

Before requesting an exchange, ask: Is the correct replacement available now? Who pays domestic return and reshipping costs? What happens if the second unit fails inspection? Weak answers make return-and-reorder the safer choice.

Understand the 5-day return window and 120-hour clock

Hipobuy's published policy describes a 5-Day Return Guarantee for eligible products. The page says a product should have been signed for within seven days and remain within five days after warehouse stock-in. It also states that the 120-hour countdown begins in the next hour after the order status becomes “Warehoused.” Treat the live account timestamp—not your memory or local time zone—as the controlling record.

Eligibility is not automatic. The policy says the seller must offer a return guarantee, the item must remain marketable and warehouse inventory time must not exceed five days. Category-specific standards may also apply: tags, seals, packaging or protective films may need to remain intact. A buyer who removes or damages them can make an otherwise eligible item harder to return.

Hipobuy also advertises a longer free-storage period for consolidation. That storage clock answers how long accepted inventory may remain in the warehouse; it does not extend the published 120-hour return clock. Inspect a newly warehoused item immediately, request missing photos the same day and keep it out of parcel selection until the decision is recorded.

If the deadline appears close, open the case first with the order number and basic evidence, then add detail requested by support. Do not wait for a planned parcel date. Once an item is packed or dispatched internationally, a domestic seller return may no longer be practical.

Calculate the published return charges before deciding

For a change-of-mind or buyer-choice return, Hipobuy's policy lists a simple charge structure: shipping back to the seller, any seller-side shipping cost and a 5 RMB service fee. Even when the original listing advertised free shipping, the seller may require repayment of the first dispatch cost. Your available account balance must be sufficient for the required charges.

Published unconditional-return formulaReturn shipping to seller + seller shipping cost + 5 RMB service fee

Responsibility changes when the evidence shows a seller-caused issue, such as the wrong item or a confirmed quality defect. The policy says the customer normally does not bear the return cost in those cases, although a seller may dispute responsibility. That is why a precise option screenshot, warehouse label and clear defect photo are more valuable than a general complaint.

The approved refund is based on the actual product price paid, not a current listing price or an assumed promotional value. Before choosing an exchange, compare domestic return cost, replacement delivery, stock certainty and the effect on your international parcel deadline.

Write seller instructions as a decision memo

The best seller message is short enough to translate accurately and complete enough to produce a decision. Use four parts: identify the order and variant; state the verified discrepancy; attach the minimum evidence; request one clear outcome.

For example: “Order 12345, selected option: grey, size L. Warehouse photos show black, size L. Please confirm whether the seller can exchange it for grey size L, whether it is in stock, and the domestic return cost before proceeding.”

Keep the message and every reply in one record. When the seller proposes a substitute, treat it as a new purchase decision. Request a link or image, exact option, price and dispatch timing. Confirm whether the replacement changes the price or adds domestic delivery.

Track refunds as money movement, not a status label

A refund is complete only when the value reaches the stated destination and can be matched to the original payment. Save the order amount, charges shown at checkout, approved refund amount, destination and completion date.

Do not assume every amount is refundable. Seller deductions, domestic return shipping, payment costs, currency conversion and coupon treatment can affect the result. Confirm the outcome for the specific order.

If the app says “refunded” but the balance or payment method does not show the expected value, save screenshots and contact support with the order number, amount and transaction references. Avoid a new payment or chargeback before confirming where the refund was sent.

Hipobuy’s landing page promotes a sign-up coupon bundle worth more than $300, but face value is not cash. Record the discount actually used and check whether cancellation restores, expires or consumes the coupon under current terms.

A repeatable return-and-exchange workflow

Use this sequence whenever a Hipobuy warehouse item raises a concern:

  1. Freeze the item from parcel selection.
  2. Retrieve the original link, selected variant, order note and payment record.
  3. Compare warehouse identity, condition, quantity and measurements.
  4. Request only the missing evidence that could change the decision.
  5. Classify the issue as seller error, condition, specification or preference.
  6. Ask support to confirm current return or exchange eligibility for that order.
  7. Compare exchange, return-and-reorder and acceptance using cost, stock and time.
  8. Send one decision-focused message with the evidence packet.
  9. Record seller response, domestic cost and expected timeline.
  10. Keep return acceptance, item dispatch and refund receipt as separate milestones.
  11. Reinspect any replacement as a new warehouse arrival.
  12. Release the item only after all five gates are passed.
  13. Build the parcel from approved inventory and live route data.
  14. Archive the order, refund and parcel records until delivery.

This workflow does not guarantee that every seller will accept a return or that every refund will be immediate. It gives the buyer a cleaner record, faster decisions and fewer accidental shipments.

Hipobuy Return Policy FAQ

Can I refund a Hipobuy order?

An eligible warehoused item may qualify under Hipobuy's published 5-day guarantee, but seller approval, product condition and category rules still matter. Open the case before the 120-hour warehouse window expires.

When does Hipobuy's return clock start?

The published policy says the 120-hour period begins in the next hour after the order becomes “Warehoused.” Use the timestamp shown in your live account.

How much does a Hipobuy return cost?

For an unconditional buyer-choice return, the published formula is return shipping to the seller, seller shipping cost and a 5 RMB service fee. Seller-caused errors are handled differently when responsibility is verified.

Does Hipobuy storage mean an item can be returned for the whole storage period?

No. Storage supports consolidation; it does not extend the separate published five-day warehouse return window.

What evidence should I provide for a wrong item?

Provide the order number, source link, selected-option screenshot, warehouse photos, labels and a concise description of the mismatch.

When is an exchange better than a return?

When the correct variant is confirmed in stock, domestic costs are acceptable and the replacement fits your parcel schedule.

Should I submit a parcel while a return case is open?

No. Freeze the affected item and verify that it is excluded until the case is closed.

How do I prove a sizing problem?

Combine the listing or seller-confirmed size information with a warehouse measurement photo showing the full tape and endpoints.

Is a seller’s substitute automatically acceptable?

No. Request the exact option, image or link, price and dispatch timing before approving it.

When is a refund actually complete?

When the approved value appears in the stated destination and matches the original order record.

Can a free exchange still increase my total cost?

Yes. Domestic return costs, replacement delivery or a second international parcel can increase the total.
