USFans Spreadsheet FAQ

What is a USFans spreadsheet?

An organized collection of product links from Chinese marketplaces that can be opened through a shopping agent.

Does every featured product open a specific page?

Yes. Each featured card opens a distinct matching product-detail URL.

Is USFansVIP an official USFans website?

No. It is an independent product-discovery and buyer-research site.

Are USFans QC photos free?

USFans publicly advertises three to seven free HD inspection photos for warehouse items.

How much is one extra QC photo?

A stable public per-photo price has not been confirmed.

Does USFans charge a service fee?

Standard purchasing is positioned as free or 0%, while other cost layers can remain.

How is billable shipping weight calculated?

Carriers generally compare actual and volumetric weight and charge according to the higher value.

Can design alone improve Google ranking?

No. Rankings also depend on useful original content, crawlability, updates and authority.
