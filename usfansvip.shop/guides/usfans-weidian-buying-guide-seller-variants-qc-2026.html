Chinese domestic freight belongs to the purchase stage. International freight depends on the later parcel, destination, route, packed weight and dimensions. A low domestic fee does not make a bulky product cheap to ship abroad. For a packaged accessory, decide whether the retail box is part of the value or unnecessary parcel volume.

A warehouse photo can confirm visible characteristics. It cannot prove long-term durability, exact material composition, comfort or authenticity. Set expectations around evidence the camera can realistically provide.
