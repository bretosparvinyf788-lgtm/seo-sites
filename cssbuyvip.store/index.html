STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions that sit between a link and a parcel.

Practical answers without pretending that one route, price or listing stays fixed forever.

Independent by designGuidance is separated from promotional claims.

A CSSBuy spreadsheet is a curated discovery layer for product listings from Chinese marketplaces. It helps you compare names, categories, prices and source links before pasting a listing into an agent. This site adds clear routes, practical checks and dated review notes instead of presenting a bare link dump.

No. CSSBuyVIP is an independent resource and is not owned by or affiliated with CSSBuy. We explain agent-shopping workflows and organize product discovery, while account, payment, warehouse and parcel services remain with the relevant providers.

Open the original marketplace listing, confirm the current variant and size, review recent seller activity where available, and treat every price as time-sensitive. After the item reaches the warehouse, compare QC images with the seller listing before accepting it for a parcel.

International shipping depends on the packed parcel, destination, route, chargeable weight and optional services. Volumetric weight can matter for bulky items. Treat early estimates as planning figures and compare the final packed measurements before submitting a parcel.

Check the correct color and size first, then compare shape, stitching, print placement, labels, hardware and visible damage. Ask for an additional angle only when the standard images do not show a decision-critical detail.

Agent warehouses commonly support consolidation, but the best combination depends on weight, dimensions, item restrictions and route limits. Removing unnecessary packaging can reduce volume, while fragile goods may need stronger protection.

W2C means where to cop. In spreadsheet communities it usually refers to the original marketplace product link. A useful W2C entry should lead to a live listing and provide enough context for the buyer to verify the correct option.

No. Marketplace sellers can change prices, stock and variants without notice. The review date tells you when an entry was last checked, but the original listing and the agent order screen are the final price references.

Plan the haul before buying, avoid mixing restricted items with ordinary goods, compare route limits, review chargeable weight and request packaging changes only when they solve a real problem. The cheapest route is not automatically the best fit.

High-interest entries should be checked frequently and every card should show a visible last-reviewed date. Dead links, major price changes and missing variants should be removed or clearly marked instead of being left to inflate the product count.
