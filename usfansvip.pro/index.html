Detailed answers

Questions that matter after the click.

A spreadsheet helps you discover an item. It does not replace checking the seller record, reviewing the warehouse arrival or understanding the route you select.

Independent site noticeUSFansVIP Pro is an independent discovery and educational site. It is not operated by USFans, KakobuyMake, Taobao, Weidian or 1688.
What is a USFans spreadsheet?

It is a curated index of product records that can help a buyer discover items from Chinese marketplaces before using a purchasing and forwarding service. This website presents category shortcuts, selected KakobuyMake records and practical buying guidance in a mobile-friendly format. It does not itself sell or authenticate the products shown.

Is USFansVIP Pro an official USFans website?

No. USFansVIP Pro is an independent external guide. USFans operates its own platform and publishes its current service, availability and promotional terms there. Treat statements on any third-party spreadsheet—including coupon values and delivery estimates—as information to verify against the live official checkout.

Where do the category and product links go?

The category and featured-product links open corresponding records on kakobuymake.com. The external catalog is responsible for its own titles, images, prices and availability. Opening a record lets you review more detail before deciding whether to use it with a purchasing service.

Does the price on a product card equal my final cost?

No. A displayed product price is only one part of the cost chain. Depending on the transaction, you may also encounter domestic delivery to the warehouse, optional services, international freight, payment conversion, insurance and destination duties or taxes. Confirm each stage before committing to a haul.

What should I check before placing the order?

Open the source record and confirm the exact variant, colour, size and quantity. Read every available measurement rather than relying on a familiar size label. Save a screenshot of the selected option and price. If a listing has several batches or versions, do not assume the cheapest and most expensive options are interchangeable.

How should I inspect warehouse QC photos?

First verify that the warehouse received the correct item and option. Then inspect colour, material appearance, stitching, print position, symmetry, hardware and obvious marks. For clothing, request or use measurement photos and compare them with a garment that already fits. For shoes, examine both sides, toe shape, sole edges and the size tag. Resolve a material problem before parcel submission.

Can QC photos prove that an item is authentic?

No. Warehouse photos are useful for confirming the received item and spotting visible defects, but photos do not establish authenticity, durability or material composition. A spreadsheet should not be treated as an authentication service. Make lawful buying decisions and respect intellectual-property and import rules in your country.

When should I return or exchange an item?

Consider a return when the received colour or size differs from the order, measurements materially miss the listing, important parts are damaged, or construction defects make the item unusable. Check the seller's return window and possible domestic freight before deciding. Small cosmetic details may be subjective; focus on issues that change fit, function or the item you actually ordered.

How do I reduce avoidable parcel volume?

Review whether retail boxes, hangers or redundant packaging are important to you. Shoes and padded bags can create substantial volumetric weight even when the scale weight is modest. Ask the forwarding service what packaging changes are available, and do not choose compression for items that could be permanently creased or damaged.

Which shipping line should I choose?

There is no universally best line. Compare only the routes currently offered for your destination and item types. Look at billed weight rules, prohibited goods, tracking quality, compensation limits, estimated ranges and destination customs treatment. The fastest advertised line is not always the best value, and an old community recommendation may no longer apply.
