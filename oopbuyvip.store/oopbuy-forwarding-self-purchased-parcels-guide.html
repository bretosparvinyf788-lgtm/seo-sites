OOPBuy Forwarding Guide: How to Send Self-Purchased Parcels to the Warehouse

A disciplined inbound workflow for buyers who pay a Chinese seller themselves and use OOPBuy for warehouse receipt and international forwarding.

Published 2026-08-2112 min read1,538 wordsFact-checked against OOPBuy official pages · August 2026

Forwarding looks simpler than proxy purchasing because you control the domestic checkout. In practice, it transfers an important responsibility from the agent to you: the warehouse must identify a parcel that OOPBuy did not purchase. If the seller ships an anonymous box, uses an unexpected tracking number or divides one order into several packages, arrival alone does not create a clean warehouse record.

OOPBuy currently presents Forwarding as a separate top-level function alongside DIY Order and its shopping-agent flow. That distinction matters. In a proxy order, the platform connects the product record, seller payment and domestic tracking. In a forwarding order, you create that bridge. The goal is not merely to send a parcel to an address. The goal is to make the physical box and your digital inbound declaration describe the same shipment.

Know when forwarding is the right workflow

Use forwarding when you can complete the domestic purchase yourself and want OOPBuy to receive the resulting parcel for later international delivery. Common reasons include buying from a seller that is difficult to parse automatically, using a private order already paid for, or moving goods you purchased through another Chinese channel.

Do not choose forwarding simply to bypass a product warning. OOPBuy’s official pages display risk reminders for professional, branded or potentially restricted goods and state that authenticity cannot always be verified. Legal restrictions and shipping-line rules still apply after a parcel reaches the warehouse. A seller’s willingness to ship domestically does not prove that an item can travel internationally.

Forwarding also changes after-sales control. Because you are the domestic purchaser, preserve the seller conversation, payment record, product description and return terms. If the wrong item arrives, OOPBuy may help document the visible situation, but the commercial relationship with the seller began outside the agent order.

Retrieve the current warehouse identity from your account

Use only the forwarding address and recipient identity displayed in your logged-in OOPBuy account at the time you create the order. Do not copy an address from a forum post, old screenshot or another user. Warehouse details, routing codes and account identifiers can change, and the identifier may be what connects the package to you.

Copy every field exactly: recipient name or code, phone number, region, street address and any required user marker. Chinese checkout forms often divide an address across province, city, district and detailed-address fields. Putting the full string into the wrong field can remove the part used for sorting.

Before paying the seller, paste the completed address back into a note and compare it character by character with the account display. This two-minute check is more valuable than trying to recover an unrecognized package later. This guide intentionally does not publish a warehouse address because the live account is the authoritative source.

Create the inbound record before dispatch

Register the forwarding order before the seller ships whenever the interface permits. A useful inbound record contains the seller or platform name, product category, quantity, expected domestic carrier, tracking number when available and a concise contents description. Avoid labels such as “stuff” or “shopping” because they cannot help reconcile an exception.

Use one line per physical parcel, not merely one line per marketplace order. If the seller promises two boxes, prepare two tracking slots. If several products will be packed into one box, record the expected combined contents. The warehouse receives cartons, while marketplaces organize transactions; your record must translate between those two systems.

Take a screenshot after submitting the inbound declaration. It should show the parcel identity you entered, not just the seller’s order confirmation. Save it beside the seller receipt and tracking page. Together, these records show what was bought, what was shipped and what OOPBuy was told to expect.

Control the domestic seller handoff

Send the seller the complete address in copyable text and ask them not to shorten the recipient code. If the order is fragile, liquid, battery-powered, magnetic or otherwise sensitive, identify that fact before dispatch rather than after arrival. Do not ask a seller to hide the contents or use a false description; obscuring the item weakens both warehouse handling and later route screening.

Request the domestic tracking number as soon as the carrier accepts the parcel. A created shipping label is not proof of movement, so distinguish “number issued” from “carrier collected.” Enter the actual number into the forwarding record and verify that its carrier matches the seller’s information.

If the seller changes carriers, update the record instead of keeping both numbers without explanation. If one number is replaced, mark the old one as cancelled. A clean tracking history lets support search for the box without guessing which identifier is live.

Handle split and merged shipments explicitly

Split shipment is the most common source of forwarding ambiguity. A seller may divide one order because items leave different locations, one product is delayed or the carton would be too large. Ask whether every parcel has its own tracking number. Register each physical package and allocate expected items to it.

The reverse situation also occurs: a seller combines multiple orders under one tracking number. Do not create several identical inbound records that make one box appear missing. Use one parcel record and list all included order references in its notes. The unit of warehouse matching is the physical delivery.

Never guess a tracking number by editing the final digit or copying a number from a previous order. If the seller cannot provide a valid carrier record, pause and obtain evidence before the box reaches the warehouse. Accuracy at dispatch is cheaper than investigation after delivery.

Reconcile delivery with warehouse intake

A domestic carrier marking a parcel delivered and OOPBuy marking it stocked are separate events. The carrier status shows handoff at an address; warehouse intake requires receipt, identification and creation of an item record. Allow for normal processing, but preserve the delivery timestamp and proof rather than relying on memory.

When the parcel appears, compare three identities: the forwarding declaration, the domestic tracking number and the warehouse record. Then compare the expected contents with the recorded item count. Resolve an unexplained difference before mixing the goods into a larger haul.

If a parcel cannot be matched, prepare a concise investigation packet: forwarding record, active tracking number, carrier delivery proof, seller shipment screenshot, expected package description and the exact account identifier used in the address. OOPBuy currently advertises round-the-clock customer support; a structured packet gives support something searchable.

Set realistic inspection expectations

OOPBuy broadly states that goods received into its warehouse undergo quality inspection and receive QC photos. Confirm the current forwarding-specific inspection and optional-photo choices in your account before assuming they are identical to proxy-purchased goods. A forwarding record may describe a parcel, but it does not guarantee every internal component will be opened or tested.

Review visible identity first: item category, color, label, quantity and obvious damage. For a sealed collectible, electronic device or custom bundle, decide whether opening would reduce value or whether inspection is necessary. Put any special handling request into the applicable service field rather than assuming the warehouse knows your preference.

QC evidence does not establish authenticity or long-term function. OOPBuy’s own risk language warns that it may be unable to authenticate certain products. Treat warehouse images as evidence of visible receipt, not a substitute for seller research or a technical test.

Move from forwarding intake to international shipping

Once every forwarded item is correctly identified, decide whether to consolidate it with agent-purchased goods or ship it separately. OOPBuy’s product flow treats warehouse-to-destination delivery as a later stage, and international options depend on actual warehouse data, destination and item characteristics.

Screen route compatibility before building the parcel. Sensitive goods may narrow the available choices. Freeze the contents, review recorded weight and dimensions, choose packing instructions and confirm that no unmatched or disputed item is included. Do not use an early estimate as a guaranteed final charge.

OOPBuy currently advertises 90 days of free storage for warehouse items. Use that period as an operating boundary, not an invitation to abandon records. Forwarded parcels are easiest to manage when each moves through a clear sequence: declared, shipped domestically, delivered, stocked, inspected, parcel-ready and submitted internationally.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between OOPBuy forwarding and proxy shopping?

With forwarding, you pay the Chinese seller yourself and create the inbound parcel record. With proxy shopping, OOPBuy places the domestic order on your behalf and connects it to the warehouse workflow.

Where should I get the OOPBuy warehouse address?

Use the current address and recipient identity displayed inside your logged-in forwarding account. Do not rely on an address copied from another buyer or an old guide.

Should one marketplace order equal one forwarding record?

Not always. Create records around physical parcels and tracking numbers. One order can split into several boxes, while several orders can be merged into one box.

What if domestic tracking says delivered but the parcel is not stocked?

Keep the delivery proof and prepare the forwarding record, tracking history, seller shipment evidence, package description and recipient identifier for support to investigate.

Does forwarding guarantee that every item can ship internationally?

No. Product restrictions, legal requirements, item characteristics and route rules still apply. Check compatibility before purchasing and again before parcel submission.
