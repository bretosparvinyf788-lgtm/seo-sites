Superbuy spreadsheet FAQ

Ten detailed answers about discovery, pricing, QC, storage, consolidation and shipping.

01What is a Superbuy spreadsheet?+

A Superbuy spreadsheet is an independently curated list of product records that helps shoppers discover items and organize seller links, prices, categories and notes before ordering through a shopping agent. It is not an official Superbuy catalogue, and each listing should be rechecked on the original marketplace before purchase.

02Is SuperBuyVIP.org affiliated with Superbuy?+

No. SuperBuyVIP.org is an independent discovery and educational resource. Superbuy names and trademarks belong to their respective owner. We link shoppers to product information and practical buying guides, but we do not process payments, purchase goods or provide official customer support.

03How do I buy a spreadsheet item through Superbuy?+

Open the product record, confirm that the seller page and available variant match the listing, then use the Superbuy link or paste the original marketplace URL into Superbuy. Select the exact size, colour and quantity, add clear notes if needed, pay for the item and domestic shipping, and wait for it to reach the warehouse for inspection.

04Are the spreadsheet prices guaranteed?+

No. Displayed prices are discovery references captured from the product record and may change because of seller edits, variant pricing, promotions, domestic freight or currency conversion. Always treat the amount shown on the current order page as the operative price before payment.

05What should I check in warehouse QC photos?+

First confirm the item, colour, size and quantity. Then inspect overall shape, symmetry, stitching, prints, hardware, stains and visible damage. Compare critical details with the seller images you saved. If a deciding area is missing, request a targeted photo or measurement before accepting the item.

06How many free QC photos does Superbuy provide?+

Superbuy's current public homepage states that three free QC photos are taken during the QC and storage stage. The exact service available for a particular order can depend on the item and current policy, so verify the order page and help centre when you purchase.

07How long can products stay in the Superbuy warehouse?+

Superbuy currently advertises 90 days of free storage in its standard shopping flow. Storage rules can vary by service or change over time, so check the warehouse countdown and the current official help information rather than relying on an older guide.

08How can I estimate international shipping?+

Use the current Superbuy shipping calculator with the destination, estimated actual weight, packed dimensions and correct product category. Test more than one packing scenario when boxes or bulky packaging are involved. The final amount can change after rehearsal packaging or an updated warehouse measurement.

09Should I ship every item in one parcel?+

Not automatically. Consolidation can reduce repeated base charges, but a restricted, fragile or bulky product may make the entire parcel harder or more expensive to ship. Group items by route eligibility, packaging compatibility, value and the level of tracking or protection you need.

10How often is this spreadsheet updated?+

Product records are reviewed in rotating batches rather than presented as permanently verified stock. Every shopper should still open the product page and confirm availability, variant, seller details and current price. Guide pages show their latest editorial update month so policy-sensitive information can be checked again.
