REVERSE PURCHASING FAQ

10 practical answers for agent-assisted buying.

Straight answers about product links, agent orders, QC photos, warehouse storage, consolidation, shipping and fees.

What is reverse purchasing through a China shopping agent?

Reverse purchasing means using an agent to buy from Chinese marketplaces on your behalf. You submit a Taobao, Weidian or 1688 product link, the agent places the domestic order, receives the item at its warehouse and later ships your consolidated parcel internationally.

How do I submit a Taobao, Weidian or 1688 product link?

Copy the original product URL and paste it into the agent search field. Before paying, confirm the seller, selected size or color, domestic shipping charge and any notes that must be passed to the purchasing team.

Does Sugargoo charge a purchasing service fee?

Standard purchases from supported mainstream platforms are generally promoted with a 0% purchasing service fee. Payment processing, seller-to-warehouse delivery, international shipping and optional warehouse services may still cost extra.

How many free QC photos are normally provided?

A standard warehouse intake commonly includes five free QC photos. They are useful for checking the visible item, but they do not replace requested measurements, functional testing or close-up photos when those checks matter.

What should I do if the item is wrong, damaged or visibly defective?

Do not submit the item for international shipping. Review the QC photos, document the issue clearly and contact support while the seller's return or exchange window is still open. Return postage or other charges can depend on the seller and the reason for the request.

How long can purchased goods stay in the warehouse?

Standard purchasing orders are commonly listed with up to 100 days of free storage. Storage rules can change by order type or promotion, so check the countdown shown in your account instead of relying only on an old guide.

Can products from different sellers be combined into one parcel?

Yes. Once eligible items have arrived at the warehouse, they can usually be consolidated into one parcel. Restricted goods, batteries, liquids, oversized products or route-specific limits may require separate packages.

How is international shipping calculated?

The charge depends on destination, route, product category, actual weight, parcel dimensions and volumetric weight. The carrier normally bills whichever chargeable weight rule applies, so removing unnecessary packaging can sometimes reduce the final amount.

What does W2C mean, and why should I verify the original link?

W2C means Where to Cop. The original marketplace link lets you verify the seller, current price, available variants and whether the listing is still active before you ask an agent to purchase it.

How can I reduce risk before approving international shipment?

Verify the live listing, compare measurements with a size chart, inspect every QC image, request essential close-ups when needed, confirm route restrictions and keep a realistic budget for payment fees, domestic delivery, packaging and international freight.
