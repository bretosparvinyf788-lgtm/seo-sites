Kakobuy FAQ: Shipping, QC Photos, Coupons & Fees

Policies and live prices can change. We show what to verify instead of pretending a static number is permanent.

W2C means “where to cop.” A W2C page helps you discover a source product on Taobao, Weidian, 1688 or Tmall. Copy that source URL, paste it into Kakobuy, then verify the seller, price, selected version, size, color and China domestic shipping before paying.
Standard warehouse QC images are generally part of the received-order workflow, but the number of included images and the current price of a special or additional photo can change. The order page shown after warehouse arrival is the source of truth. KakobuyLab deliberately does not publish a permanent “one extra photo costs X” claim without a stable official fee page.
Ask for one decisive angle or measurement: insole length beside a ruler, a close-up of a suspected stain, the rear alignment of both shoes, a care label, hardware engraving, or a bag laid flat with width measured. “Take more photos” wastes time; a precise instruction produces evidence you can actually use.
There is no universal rate. Your final quote depends on destination, shipping line, actual weight, volumetric weight, item restrictions, parcel dimensions, optional insurance and current carrier pricing. Use our estimator only for planning, then compare eligible live routes in the Kakobuy parcel screen.
Kakobuy currently promotes “Pay Only €3 Per Parcel.” Treat that as a fee headline, not a promise that every other cost disappears. Product price, seller-to-warehouse shipping, international freight, optional services, payment conversion and possible tax or duty remain separate components.
The official site promotes a $410 new-user coupon bundle. A bundle normally means multiple vouchers with minimum-spend, route, timing or user-status conditions—not $410 taken from one ordinary parcel. Open the coupon wallet and check the threshold, expiry and eligible shipping line for each voucher.
These are the main source-link types used in W2C shopping. Kakobuy may parse the page automatically or require manual order details. Always compare the parsed title, SKU image, color, size, quantity and price with the original page before submitting payment.
Aircraft and trucks sell space as well as kilograms. A light but bulky shoebox may occupy more capacity than its scale weight suggests, so some lines use length × width × height divided by a route-specific divisor. Rehearsal packing, box removal and careful consolidation can reduce the measured volume.
Sometimes a route is described as tax-free or duty-handled, while other routes leave import charges to the recipient. The answer depends on the destination and exact line. Read the live route description and declared-value rules rather than relying on a general social-media answer.
