Source-link guide

Kakobuy W2C: from source link to warehouse.

W2C is not just a product URL. It is a chain of evidence connecting the discovery page, Chinese marketplace listing, selected SKU, Kakobuy order and warehouse QC record.

Reviewed September 22, 2026~11 minute readKakobuyLab Research Team

“W2C” means “where to cop.” In shopping-agent communities, the phrase asks for the source of an item, usually a Taobao, Weidian, 1688 or Tmall product page. A reliable W2C workflow preserves the exact source and variation until the item reaches the warehouse. A bad workflow loses information between screenshots, spreadsheets and auto-translated checkout fields.

What a W2C link actually does

A source link lets the buying agent identify a seller’s listing in China. Kakobuy uses that page to display available variations, collect payment and purchase on the customer’s behalf. The product is sent to a domestic warehouse, photographed, stored and later combined into an international parcel. This separates the seller transaction from international delivery.

The link is therefore the beginning, not the conclusion. It may point to a listing with dozens of colors, sizes and versions. Its headline price may represent the cheapest variation or a deposit. The seller may charge domestic freight. Some pages can be parsed automatically; others need a manual order. A good W2C page helps you preserve the details that the raw URL alone cannot explain.

KakobuyLab uses direct product entries from KakobuyMake as discovery paths. Each entry should lead back to a source record, while the final purchase and live policy check happen on Kakobuy. This separation is useful for search: discovery content can explain the item and buying process without pretending to be the checkout platform.

Taobao, Weidian, 1688 and Tmall are not interchangeable

Taobao

Taobao is a broad consumer marketplace with individual stores, factories, resellers and established merchants. Listings often contain many SKU images and promotional options. Product descriptions can be long, and the first displayed price may change after a variation is selected. When Kakobuy parses a Taobao link, confirm that the displayed SKU labels and images match the original page.

Weidian

Weidian is common in community-sourced fashion and footwear finds. Listings can be brief, mobile-oriented and seller-specific. A single store may publish several batches or versions with similar titles. Keep the seller name, product title, selected version and SKU screenshot together. A copied link without its surrounding notes can become ambiguous after the seller edits the page.

1688

1688 is oriented toward domestic wholesale and manufacturing supply. Listings may show tiered pricing, minimum quantities or packaging assumptions. The lowest number can apply only above a quantity threshold. Before ordering, verify whether one unit is allowed, whether the price changes by quantity, and whether color or size mixing is permitted. Domestic freight may also be calculated differently from a consumer marketplace.

Tmall

Tmall generally hosts more structured brand and retail storefronts, but the agent workflow still requires exact SKU verification. Official-store presentation does not remove the need to check color, model number, size and included components. Promotions can depend on timing or membership and may not transfer directly through an agent purchase.

How to evaluate a W2C find before copying it

Start with the date and source. A spreadsheet card can remain indexed after a seller changes or removes the original listing. Open the product record and compare the current title, price and images. If the source is unavailable, do not assume a replacement link represents the same version. A visual match is not proof of the same seller, material or batch.

Check whether the discovery page distinguishes item price from delivered cost. A low product price can be offset by domestic freight, heavy packaging or international volume. A bulky low-cost jacket may cost more to ship than a denser expensive accessory. W2C research should include approximate weight and dimensions whenever they materially affect the decision.

Look for variant clarity. Useful listings show a unique image or label for every version. Risky listings use vague labels such as “upgraded,” “top,” or numbered options without a stable reference. Save the selected SKU thumbnail and its original-language label. Auto-translation can turn similar Chinese option names into the same English phrase.

Source trail: keep five things together—source URL, seller or store, selected SKU image, selected SKU text, and the price visible after selection. That compact record can resolve most parsing or warehouse disputes.

Paste the link into Kakobuy without losing the SKU

Copy the clean marketplace product URL when possible. Remove unrelated social redirect wrappers, tracking pages and link shorteners. Paste the link into Kakobuy and wait for the listing to parse. Compare the displayed product image and title with the source. Then select the exact color, size, version and quantity.

Do not pay immediately just because the page loaded. Confirm whether the agent’s displayed price updated after the SKU selection. Review China domestic freight. Read seller notes about preorder timing, exchanges, custom products or no-return conditions. If the source offers a size chart, save it before the listing changes.

If the page fails to parse, a manual order may require the source URL, item name, unit price, selected attributes, quantity and a reference image. Enter the original-language option text alongside an English note where possible. The purchasing team needs unambiguous instructions, not a paragraph of preferences. Put optional questions in the remark field and keep mandatory SKU details in the designated attributes.

Why the displayed W2C price may not be the checkout price

Marketplace listings often use a price range. The lowest figure may belong to a small accessory, deposit, child size or base version. Promotional prices may require a marketplace coupon that an agent account cannot apply. The seller can also update the price between discovery and purchase. The authoritative product cost is the amount attached to the selected SKU when Kakobuy processes the order.

Some orders trigger a price adjustment after the purchasing team contacts the seller. An increase is not automatically suspicious, but it should be explained by the chosen option, domestic shipping or seller update. Compare the adjustment with your saved source record. If the item is no longer available, reject a substitute unless you explicitly accept its seller and specification.

Currency conversion adds another layer. A spreadsheet may display a converted US-dollar amount using one rate, while Kakobuy wallet funding uses another rate or payment method spread. Use source-page CNY as the stable comparison unit, then treat the converted amount as an estimate until payment.

Assess seller risk without inventing certainty

Store age, sales volume, buyer feedback, return terms and detailed original photos can reduce uncertainty, but no single indicator guarantees a good item. A highly rated store can still send the wrong SKU. A new store can sell a straightforward product successfully. Focus on evidence relevant to the item: clear variation photos, consistent measurements, transparent materials and workable returns.

Be cautious when a W2C page copies images without preserving the seller link. Reverse searching a photo may reveal several listings at different prices, but those listings may represent different materials or factories. Choose a source because its terms and evidence are understandable, not only because its headline price is lowest.

Community reviews are most useful when they show a complete path: source listing, order date, warehouse photos, measured dimensions and received-item feedback. A cropped glamour photo without a source trail tells you little about the link you are about to use.

Understand the order status sequence

After payment, an order typically moves through purchasing, seller dispatch, domestic transit, warehouse arrival and quality-control processing. Status wording can vary, but each stage answers a different question. “Purchased” means the agent placed the order, not that the seller shipped. “Seller sent” begins domestic transit. “Stored” or an equivalent warehouse status means the physical item was processed into your account.

Delays can occur before seller dispatch, particularly for preorder or custom items. Decide in advance how long you are willing to wait. If a seller misses the promised window, ask for a cancellation only after checking whether the marketplace transaction permits it. Repeated status requests do not make a preorder ready faster, but a clear deadline protects consolidation plans.

Warehouse arrival is where the W2C source trail meets reality. Compare the QC gallery with the selected SKU image and notes. Verify size, color, quantity and visible construction. If the item differs, use the saved source evidence when requesting an exchange or return. This is why a clean source record matters.

How W2C and QC work together before shipping

The W2C record tells you what should arrive: the seller, listing, selected SKU, price and any promised accessories. The QC gallery shows what actually reached the warehouse. Compare those two records before judging smaller cosmetic details. A correct-looking item can still be the wrong size or version, while a misleading listing image can make a correct warehouse item appear questionable.

Start with identity: confirm item type, color, size, quantity and version against the saved source record. Then inspect visible condition, construction and the measurements that affect use or fit. If one important detail is missing, request a precise extra photo or measurement while the seller return window is still open. Do not authorize international shipping simply because the overall photo looks acceptable.

Once the item matches the source and passes QC, use its warehouse weight and packaging to plan the parcel. This sequence keeps each decision based on evidence: the source page defines the order, QC verifies the received item, and the parcel quote determines whether the final delivered cost still makes sense.

Common W2C mistakes

  1. Copying a screenshot instead of preserving the clickable source URL.
  2. Selecting an English auto-translation without checking the SKU image.
  3. Assuming the lowest displayed price applies to the desired version.
  4. Ignoring domestic freight, minimum quantity or preorder status.
  5. Replacing an unavailable listing with a visually similar but unverified seller.
  6. Paying before saving the size chart and source-page evidence.
  7. Forgetting to compare warehouse QC with the selected SKU record.
  8. Judging total value from item price without estimating international shipping.

A repeatable W2C workflow

Discover the item, open the source, verify the seller, select the exact SKU, save the source trail and calculate a rough delivered-cost range. Paste the clean URL into Kakobuy, compare the parsed information, add concise remarks and pay only after the variant and price are correct. Monitor seller dispatch, inspect warehouse QC against the original record, then decide whether to keep, exchange or return before parcel submission.

The best W2C workflow is not the fastest possible click path. It is the shortest path that preserves enough evidence to recover when a listing changes, a translation fails or the warehouse receives the wrong item.
