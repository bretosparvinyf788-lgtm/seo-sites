How to Reduce Kakobuy Shipping Cost

A lower quote should not create a higher loss risk. Inspect first, consolidate carefully, then optimize packaging and route.

01 · Inspect

Resolve QC problems

Do not pay international freight for an item you already plan to return. Finish measurements and defect checks while the item is still in the warehouse.

02 · Pack

Control parcel volume

Use rehearsal packing when available. Remove unnecessary retail boxes only if you accept the reduced protection and resale impact.

03 · Compare

Read route details

Compare transit range, weight rules, restrictions, insurance, tax handling and tracking—not just the lowest number in the list.

Kakobuy Volumetric Weight: Why 2 kg Can Bill at 3.5 kg

A parcel measuring 42 × 30 × 17 cm has 21,420 cm³ of volume. With a 6,000 divisor, dimensional weight is 3.57 kg. If the line bills the greater of dimensional and actual weight, 3.57 kg—not 2 kg—drives the charge. The exact divisor and rounding step vary by line.
