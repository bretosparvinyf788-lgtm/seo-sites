Superbuy Coupons & New-User Discounts

Read the individual coupon terms instead of treating a headline bundle as cash.

Superbuy has advertised a CN¥560 new-user shipping coupon bundle. A bundle is a collection of conditional coupons, not a CN¥560 cash balance.

Check five conditions

  1. Confirm whether the account qualifies as a new user.
  2. Record the individual coupon expiration date.
  3. Check the minimum international-shipping spend.
  4. Confirm whether the selected route accepts the coupon.
  5. Check whether the coupon can be stacked with another discount.

Compare discounted totals, not coupon face value

A large coupon attached to an expensive route may still produce a higher final cost than a smaller discount on a more suitable line. First remove routes that cannot accept the parcel. Then compare eligible routes after discount, using the same packed dimensions and product attributes.

Task rewards are separate

New-user activities can sometimes offer additional shipping coupons for completing beginner steps, submitting a first order, using a value-added service, sending a first international parcel or sharing. These rewards should be evaluated separately from the headline bundle because each may carry its own expiry and minimum-spend threshold.
