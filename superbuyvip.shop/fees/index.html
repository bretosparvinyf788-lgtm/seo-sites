Superbuy Fees & Total Cost

Separate the basic purchasing fee from every other cost before you decide what an order or parcel really costs.

Updated September 11, 2026Independent guide

The clearest way to understand Superbuy fees is to separate the buying stage, warehouse stage, parcel stage and destination stage. “No service fee” describes only one line of that ledger.

The four-part cost model

StageTypical cost linesControl point
PurchaseProduct price, Chinese domestic delivery, platform-specific purchasing service.Verify the listing, variation and seller terms before payment.
PaymentPayment-channel costs and currency conversion.Compare the total amount charged, not only the displayed product price.
WarehouseOptional photos, re-inspection, video, packaging changes and storage after the free period.Buy only services that answer a decision or reduce a named risk.
ParcelInternational freight, insurance, route add-ons and possible destination charges.Compare chargeable weight and the complete landed-cost pathway.

Basic purchasing is platform-dependent

Public Superbuy information lists a CN¥0 standard purchasing service fee for mainstream sources such as Taobao, Tmall, JD.com and 1688. Special or secondhand platforms can use different rules. The source platform is therefore a cost field, not just a link. Record it when you build the order budget.

Optional warehouse services are decision tools

A detailed photo is useful when it answers one decisive question: a measurement, label, model number, missing part or visible defect. Re-inspection can help when the standard evidence does not resolve a discrepancy. Video, try-on or packaging options may be appropriate for specific goods, but ordering every add-on by default makes the cost ledger noisy.

Storage can turn delay into cost

Public guidance lists 90 days of free warehouse storage after arrival. Because items reach the warehouse on different dates, a haul does not share one deadline. Keep a row for each order with stocked-in date, return deadline, QC status and latest planned parcel date. Consolidation saves repeated shipping charges only when the waiting cost and deadline risk remain acceptable.

International shipping is an estimate before packing

The parcel estimate depends on destination, item attributes, weight, dimensions and route. Final packing establishes the carton measurements; the logistics provider’s bill can create a refund or additional payment. Keep a reconciliation margin and compare more than one scenario in the shipping calculator.

Build a one-page cost ledger

  1. Record product price and Chinese domestic delivery for every order line.
  2. Add payment and currency-conversion cost from the actual transaction.
  3. Assign optional services to the exact item that needed them.
  4. Allocate storage cost by stocked-in date if the free period is exceeded.
  5. Model international freight using realistic packed dimensions.
  6. Add insurance and possible receiver-side charges separately.
  7. Subtract only coupons that are valid for the chosen route.

Three false savings to avoid

Skipping decisive evidence: saving a small photo fee can be expensive when the wrong size becomes internationally non-returnable. Removing all packaging: lower volume is not a saving if fragile or structured goods arrive damaged. Choosing only the lowest estimate: an ineligible or unsuitable route is not a usable price.

Budget rule: every fee should belong to a stage and a decision. If a cost line cannot be explained, verify it before paying rather than hiding it inside “shipping.”

Frequently asked questions

Does zero purchasing fee mean the whole order is free of extra costs?

No. It refers to the listed basic purchasing service on eligible platforms. Product price, domestic delivery, payment, optional warehouse services, international shipping and destination charges remain separate.

Are the prices on this page permanent?

No. They are a dated public snapshot. The amount displayed in the live service or checkout flow is the final reference.

Which optional service is most useful?

The useful service is the one that resolves a material risk. A focused detail photo, for example, can be worthwhile when it decides whether you keep or return an item.
