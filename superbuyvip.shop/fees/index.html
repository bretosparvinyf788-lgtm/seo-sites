Superbuy Service Fees & Optional Costs

Separate the basic purchasing fee from product cost, domestic delivery, optional warehouse services, international shipping and destination charges.

“No service fee” does not mean an order has no additional costs. It means the standard purchasing service on certain mainstream platforms is publicly listed at zero.

Storage

Public guidance lists 90 days of free storage after warehouse arrival, followed by a daily per-item charge. Storage extension can increase the maximum time but does not necessarily remove charges accrued after the free period. Track each item by its own stocked-in date.

Costs outside the service-fee label

  • Product price and Chinese domestic shipping.
  • Payment-channel or exchange-rate costs.
  • Optional inspection, photography and packaging.
  • International shipping and insurance.
  • Destination tax, customs or carrier charges where applicable.
Price-review date: July 30, 2026. Public pages can contain older examples, so the price shown in live checkout remains the final reference.
