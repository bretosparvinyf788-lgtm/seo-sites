Where to Cop workflow

Superbuy W2C: Find, Verify, Order

A useful W2C result is more than a copied link. It should preserve the seller, variation, price snapshot, link-check date, QC status and likely parcel impact.

W2C means “Where to Cop.” In a Superbuy workflow, the practical answer is normally a usable Taobao, Tmall, Weidian, 1688 or other Chinese marketplace listing that can be purchased, inspected and shipped.

Start with source-link intelligence

Products circulate through screenshots, short videos, spreadsheets, seller albums and community comments. The first task is not clicking buy; it is determining whether the listing still represents the product shown. Check the platform, seller identity, current variation menu, domestic shipping terms and image consistency. A reseller may use the same photos at a different price, and the cheapest visible option may be an accessory or deposit rather than the complete product.

Build a record that survives a dead link

FieldWhy it matters
Original platform and sellerDistinguishes the source from reposts and resellers.
Last link checkShows whether the entry is actively maintained.
Price snapshotProvides context without pretending the price is permanent.
Variation notePrevents ordering the wrong size, color, model or bundle.
QC statusSeparates a raw find from an item with warehouse evidence.
Parcel impactFlags bulky, fragile, dense or route-restricted goods.

Use honest labels. “Link checked” means the page loaded on a stated date. “QC available” means warehouse images exist for a specific version. “Editorial pick” means the item is included by the directory, not that quality is guaranteed.

Diagnose the listing before submission

Read the variation menu slowly. Translated labels can hide bundle differences, presale options, deposits or accessory-only selections. Compare option text, thumbnail and price. For 1688, minimum order quantities and mixed-batch rules may matter. When automatic capture fails, enter the link, product name, category, price, size, color and remarks manually in the agent order form.

Write remarks as operating instructions

Useful remarks tell the purchasing agent what to do: confirm the selected size before payment, cancel if the exact color is unavailable, ask whether an accessory is included, or contact you if the seller changes the price above a stated limit. Avoid asking an agent to guarantee subjective quality from a marketplace photo.

Connect discovery to warehouse evidence

After the item arrives, compare the standard QC photos with the saved listing and selected variation. A saved record helps prove a wrong label, color or configuration even if the seller later edits the listing. Request one detailed photo only when a missing measurement or visible detail could change the keep-or-return decision.

Include parcel impact before ordering

A low-priced product can become an expensive haul addition when it is bulky, fragile, boxed, liquid, battery-powered or magnetic. One special item may remove cheaper shipping routes from an otherwise ordinary clothing parcel. A strong W2C entry links naturally to QC guidance and shipping planning.

Repeatable W2C checklist

  1. Identify the likely original listing rather than the nearest repost.
  2. Confirm platform, seller, price, domestic shipping and variation structure.
  3. Save a dated product record before ordering.
  4. Enter exact size, color, model, quantity and fallback instructions.
  5. Track seller dispatch and respond to purchasing enquiries.
  6. Compare warehouse photos with the saved order record.
  7. Approve, return or exchange before after-sales options narrow.
  8. Assess weight, volume, fragility and route restrictions before consolidation.
W2C principle: the best result is not the link that ends the search fastest. It is the record that reduces ambiguity at every later stage.
