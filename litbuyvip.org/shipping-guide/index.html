LitBuy Shipping Guide: Weight, Volume & Route Choice

International shipping cost depends on the parcel that actually exists. Compare scale weight with dimensional weight, remove low-value bulk only when protection remains adequate, and choose a route that accepts every declared parcel attribute.

Estimate dimensional weight from outer carton length × width × height divided by the route divisor.
