QC PHOTO METHOD

LitBuy QC Finder: A Practical Photo Checklist

QC photographs are decision evidence, not decoration. Check identity first, then overall form, construction lines, fine details and measurements. Ask for one targeted image only when it can change the accept, question or return decision.

01

Identity

Match the model, color, size label, quantity and included pieces against the saved order record.

02

Structure

Review shape, alignment, paired-item symmetry, seams, edges, closures and obvious surface damage.

03

Measurement

Request a ruler or scale reference when fit, dimensions or weight materially affect the decision.
