Corrections and feedback

Contact CSSBuyVip

Use this page for website feedback, factual corrections and reports of broken or changed product destinations.

What to include

Please identify the affected CSSBuyVip page, the exact statement or destination that needs review, the date you checked it and any public evidence that supports the correction. Clear reports help the site owner verify and update pages faster.

Correction requests

When contacting the site owner through the channel where this website was shared, include the affected page URL and evidence in one message. A monitored public inbox will be listed here only after it has been configured and verified.

CSSBuy account or order support

CSSBuyVip cannot access CSSBuy accounts, payments, warehouse records or parcels. Questions about a specific order must be sent through CSSBuy’s own support channels.
