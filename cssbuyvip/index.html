CSSBuy Spreadsheet FAQ

What can CSSBuy help me buy?

CSSBuy helps users purchase from Chinese marketplaces such as Taobao, Tmall, JD, 1688 and Weidian. It also offers Buy For Me, Ship For Me and seller-contact services.

How does CSSBuy estimate international shipping?

The cost calculator uses product weight, package volume, commodity attributes and destination country to show available shipping methods, transit time, fees and restrictions.

Does CSSBuy provide QC photos?

CSSBuy can provide warehouse quality inspection images after items arrive, helping users check appearance, size and visible details before shipping.

How long can items stay in the CSSBuy warehouse?

Items can usually stay in the warehouse for a limited free-storage period, and users should check current warehouse rules before delaying shipment.

Can CSSBuy help with returns or seller questions?

CSSBuy can contact sellers on your behalf and may help with returns when the seller accepts and return conditions are met.

What is included in this CSSBuy spreadsheet?

The page contains 10 reviewed finds with source destinations, real product images, visible prices, QC checks and shipping notes. Always open the current listing and verify the details before ordering.

Are the W2C and product links guaranteed to stay live?

No. Marketplace listings, prices and stock can change without notice. Treat each link as a discovery starting point and recheck the seller, variant, price and availability before payment.

How should I use the spreadsheet to plan a haul?

Shortlist products, compare prices and seller details, record the exact variant, review warehouse QC photos, then estimate weight, volume and route restrictions before submitting a parcel.

Does CSSBuyVip sell products or process orders?

No. CSSBuyVip is an independent discovery and buyer-guide site. Product and category links open the relevant external destination where availability, payment and order handling are controlled.

When was this spreadsheet last reviewed?

The current homepage review is dated September 2026. Individual listings can change sooner, so confirm every product page again when you are ready to order.
