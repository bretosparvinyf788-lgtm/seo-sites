CSSBuy Spreadsheet FAQ

What can CSSBuy help me buy?

CSSBuy helps users purchase from Chinese marketplaces such as Taobao, Tmall, JD, 1688 and Weidian. It also offers Buy For Me, Ship For Me and seller-contact services.

How does CSSBuy estimate international shipping?

The cost calculator uses product weight, package volume, commodity attributes and destination country to show available shipping methods, transit time, fees and restrictions.

Does CSSBuy provide QC photos?

CSSBuy can provide warehouse quality inspection images after items arrive, helping users check appearance, size and visible details before shipping.

How long can items stay in the CSSBuy warehouse?

Items can usually stay in the warehouse for a limited free-storage period, and users should check current warehouse rules before delaying shipment.

Can CSSBuy help with returns or seller questions?

CSSBuy can contact sellers on your behalf and may help with returns when the seller accepts and return conditions are met.
