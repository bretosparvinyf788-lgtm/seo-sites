Privacy and Cookie Notice

This notice explains the limited information used to operate and improve CSSBuyVip.

Analytics

CSSBuyVip uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate page visits and actions such as opening the finds page, selecting a language, reading a guide or following a product destination. Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers according to Google’s settings and policies.

Local preferences

The language selector stores the chosen language in browser local storage so the preference can remain on the same device. CSSBuyVip does not use that preference to identify a person.

Outbound destinations

Links can lead to other websites. Those destinations operate under their own privacy and cookie policies. CSSBuyVip does not receive a visitor’s CSSBuy login, order, payment or warehouse information.

Contact

Messages sent by email contain the information the sender chooses to provide. Use the contact page for privacy questions or correction requests.
