Privacy Policy

This policy explains the limited information used by HipoBuyVIP.net, an independent static guide and product-discovery website.

Effective and last updated: July 30, 2026

1. Information HipoBuyVIP does not need

HipoBuyVIP does not operate buyer accounts, place orders, receive product payments, manage warehouse inventory, or process international parcels. Visitors should not submit names, addresses, telephone numbers, identity documents, payment details, account passwords, private tracking records, or order numbers to this website.

2. Language preference stored in your browser

The homepage language switcher may save a preference in browser storage under the key hipobuyvip-language. The selected language may also appear in the page URL as a lang parameter. This information is used only to display the selected website language on the same device and browser.

3. Technical and security logs

Hosting, content-delivery, domain, and security providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, response status, date, time, and security signals. These logs may be used to deliver the website, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and protect the service.

The static HipoBuyVIP pages do not intentionally set advertising cookies. Third-party websites opened from product, marketplace, support, or reference links may use their own cookies, analytics, accounts, and privacy practices. HipoBuyVIP does not control those third-party services.

5. Public support and correction requests

The contact page may direct visitors to a public GitHub issue tracker for editorial corrections, broken links, technical problems, rights notices, or privacy requests. Content posted there is publicly visible. Do not include confidential or personally identifying information.

6. Outbound links

Links may lead to marketplaces, product pages, buyer platforms, hosting services, and other external websites. Their privacy policies apply once you leave HipoBuyVIP. Review the destination website before creating an account or submitting information.

7. Data retention and control

You can remove the saved language preference by clearing this website's browser storage. Infrastructure logs are controlled by the relevant hosting and security providers and may be retained for operational, legal, and security purposes according to their own policies.

8. Children’s privacy

HipoBuyVIP is a general informational website and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit a child’s personal information through public support channels.

9. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when website functions, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are published.

10. Privacy questions

Use the contact page for a privacy question, correction, or removal request. Do not place private account or transaction details in a public issue.

Important: HipoBuyVIP cannot access or delete data held by HipoBuy, KakobuyMake, a marketplace seller, a payment provider, a shipping carrier, or another third party. Contact that service directly for account-level requests.
