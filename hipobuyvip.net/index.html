Reverse purchasing help

HipoBuy Reverse Purchasing FAQ

Ten practical answers covering product links, agent purchasing, warehouse QC, returns, consolidation, shipping, restrictions and import costs.

What is reverse purchasing and how does HipoBuy work?+
Reverse purchasing means using an agent to buy products from Chinese marketplaces on your behalf. You submit a product link, pay the product and domestic-delivery amount, wait for warehouse arrival and inspection, then choose international shipping after the items are ready.
How do I submit a Taobao, 1688 or Weidian product link?+
Copy the original product URL and paste it into the HipoBuy search or order field. Before paying, confirm the selected colour, size, quantity, seller price and any notes that the purchasing team must follow.
What happens after I pay for a product?+
The purchasing team places the domestic order with the seller. After the seller ships, the item is received at the warehouse, registered to your account and prepared for QC review before you decide whether to keep, return or ship it.
What should I check in warehouse QC photos?+
First verify identity: product, colour, size and quantity. Then inspect visible construction such as stitching, print placement, shape, hardware, stains and damage. QC photos show visible evidence only; they do not prove authenticity, long-term durability or hidden material quality.
Can I request extra QC photos or measurements?+
Yes, when the standard photos do not show a decision-critical area. Request one precise angle or measurement, such as chest width, insole length, size tag or bag interior. Any current charge or allowance should be checked inside the live order interface.
Can I return or exchange an item before international shipping?+
A domestic return or exchange may be possible while the item is still in the warehouse, subject to the seller's return policy, timing and domestic shipping costs. Submit the request quickly and review the current after-sales conditions before confirming.
How long can products remain in the HipoBuy warehouse?+
HipoBuy's public app information promotes up to 90 days of free storage. Use this period to coordinate arrivals and consolidate a planned parcel, but check the live account rules because storage terms and overdue handling can change.
Can I combine products from different sellers into one parcel?+
Yes, consolidation is a core agent workflow. Items from different sellers can be stored and packed together after they arrive and pass inspection. Keep restricted, fragile or high-volume items separate when combining them would reduce route options or increase damage risk.
How are international shipping cost and volumetric weight calculated?+
Shipping depends on destination, eligible line, actual weight, parcel dimensions, volumetric weight and packing choices. Many lines charge the greater of actual and dimensional weight, so removing unnecessary packaging can reduce cost, but the live packed quotation is the final reference.
Which products may be restricted, and who pays customs or taxes?+
Liquids, perfume, batteries, magnetic items, powders and some branded or sensitive goods may have limited routes. Import taxes and customs handling depend on the destination and selected line; the recipient is generally responsible unless the chosen service explicitly includes tax handling.
