USFans Package Forwarding Guide 2026: Register Tracking Numbers, Declarations and Warehouse Exceptions

FORWARD ORDER FILE

Connect every self-purchased parcel to one private tracking number, accurate declaration and documented warehouse intake.

USFans package forwarding is useful when you buy an item yourself on a Chinese platform and need the warehouse to receive it before international shipment. It also removes one layer of control. In a normal agent purchase, the purchasing order already connects the seller, product variation, payment and domestic tracking. With a self-purchased parcel, you must create that connection accurately.

The forwarding record is therefore not a casual delivery note. It is the ownership and intake file for the package. A missing tracking number, combined shipment, cash-on-delivery fee, false value or vague product description can turn a valid purchase into an unidentified or rejected warehouse arrival. The right workflow starts before the seller dispatches and continues until the parcel is weighed, stored and matched to the correct account.

Understand what forwarding changes

A Forward Order does not ask USFans to purchase the goods. You handle the seller transaction, listing choice, variation, Chinese domestic payment and seller communication. USFans receives the package at its warehouse, performs the forwarding intake process and later lets you combine or submit eligible items for international delivery. This means seller-side mistakes remain your problem to document and resolve.

Before buying, confirm that the Chinese seller can send to the dedicated warehouse address and that domestic freight is prepaid. USFans explicitly states that it supports prepaid-freight forwarding parcels only. Cash-on-delivery packages are refused and returned to the seller. A low-cost product is not a bargain if the seller ships with an unpaid delivery charge that the warehouse will not accept.

Obtain and copy the dedicated address correctly

USFans asks users to verify the registered phone number before obtaining a dedicated forwarding address. Treat the displayed address as structured data. Copy the recipient name, phone number, province, city, district, street details and any account-identifying code exactly as shown. Do not translate, shorten or rearrange a field because it looks repetitive.

Send the seller the complete address in one message, then compare the seller’s shipping label or order detail with your saved copy. A missing code may prevent automatic account matching even when the street address is correct. Store the address version used for the purchase, because an old address saved in a marketplace account can silently replace the current warehouse details at checkout.

Create one record for each domestic tracking number

USFans’ forwarding agreement says every tracking number requires a separate order. This rule matters when one marketplace order becomes several physical packages. A seller may split colors, send stock from different locations or add a replacement under another courier number. One product order is not necessarily one warehouse parcel.

Create the Forward Order before dispatch whenever possible. Record the Chinese carrier, full tracking number, package name, product quantity, total product value and useful remarks. If the seller later adds another tracking number, create another forwarding record rather than editing the first into a combined description. The warehouse scans physical courier labels, so your records must follow packages, not the seller’s checkout number.

Protect the tracking number as ownership evidence

USFans warns users not to disclose the tracking number because it is the primary identifier proving that a forwarded parcel belongs to them. This is more than a privacy preference. A self-purchased parcel arrives without a USFans purchasing order inside it, so the courier number is the cleanest bridge between the external seller and the account.

Keep the number in your private order ledger with the seller order ID, product name and recipient address. Share it only through the relevant account record or an official support case when necessary. If the carrier changes the number during transfer, retain both the original and final numbers plus a screenshot showing their relationship. Never post an active warehouse tracking number in a public group while asking for delivery advice.

Submit accurate product and value information

The Forward Order asks for product information, quantity and total value. USFans tells users to truthfully declare the total goods value to reduce package rejection or customs inspection risk. Enter the actual merchandise value represented by that physical parcel, not the full value of a marketplace order that was split into several boxes.

Use a plain product description that identifies what is inside. “Clothing” is more useful than a seller nickname, while “cotton hoodies, three pieces” is better than “gift.” Describe batteries, liquids, magnets, food or fragile components honestly because these details influence screening and route eligibility. Accurate information cannot guarantee customs release, but inaccurate information creates a preventable conflict between the record, the inspected contents and the later international declaration.

Meet the forwarding information deadline

USFans’ forwarding agreement says logistics information should be submitted within 15 days. Do not interpret this as permission to wait until the last day. The safest sequence is to create the Forward Order as soon as the seller provides a valid tracking number and then monitor it alongside the carrier record.

Early registration gives the warehouse a record to match when the package arrives. Late registration creates an avoidable unidentified-parcel problem. If a seller dispatches without sharing the courier number, contact the seller immediately. If tracking is replaced, update or add the correct record before the final delivery scan and preserve evidence of the change.

Know what happens at warehouse receiving

The official forwarding rules say severely damaged outer packaging or a parcel that fails acceptance standards may be refused or returned directly. An accepted parcel is opened for prohibited-item screening before it is weighed and stored. If the contents appear unsuitable for transportation or air transport, the order receives a special mark or alert for the buyer to handle.

These checks are intake controls, not a complete product-authentication service. Review the first warehouse status and images promptly. Confirm that the outer package, apparent product type and quantity are plausible. If an alert appears, read the stated reason and respond before attempting consolidation. Do not submit an abnormal item with ordinary goods simply to see whether a route appears.

Respect the limits of forwarding quality inspection

The forwarding agreement describes a narrower free-inspection scope than many buyers expect. It says color variance, sizing discrepancies and performance effects are not covered. For professional goods such as electronics, digital products and peripherals, quality, model and accessories are outside the stated inspection coverage. Sealed products such as CDs and figure models are not opened; only external damage can be checked.

The same terms say the free forwarding QC does not guarantee exact quantities, colors or sizes. This makes seller-side evidence essential. Save variation screenshots, packing lists and messages before dispatch. If the warehouse evidence cannot answer a material question, request a focused additional check where available or return the parcel according to the seller agreement. Do not treat a stored status as proof of functionality, authenticity or complete accessory count.

Handle cancellation and abnormal parcels promptly

A forwarding order cannot simply be canceled after the warehouse has signed for the package. USFans says the buyer can wait for intake and then arrange return shipment to the original seller or another address. If you already agreed with the seller that delivery should be rejected, the platform instructs you to contact support to establish a rejection notice before receipt.

The forwarding agreement also states that abnormal parcels are stored for 60 days. Treat that as an exception-resolution period, not extra free time. Record the alert date, identify whether the issue is ownership, packaging, prohibited contents or transportation suitability, and choose the permitted resolution. Delaying can reduce seller-return options and make evidence harder to obtain.

A twelve-step USFans forwarding intake routine

  1. Verify the product is legal and eligible before self-purchase.
  2. Obtain the current dedicated address after phone verification.
  3. Copy every address field and account identifier exactly.
  4. Require the seller to prepay Chinese domestic freight.
  5. Create one Forward Order per physical tracking number.
  6. Record the product description, quantity and truthful parcel value.
  7. Keep tracking numbers private and preserve number changes.
  8. Submit logistics information early and within the stated period.
  9. Monitor seller handling separately from domestic carrier transit.
  10. Review warehouse receiving, screening and exception alerts promptly.
  11. Compare stored weight, dimensions, packaging and limited QC evidence.
  12. Resolve abnormal goods before international parcel consolidation.

Frequently asked questions about USFans package forwarding

What is a USFans Forward Order?

It is the warehouse record used to identify and receive a parcel that you purchased yourself from a Chinese seller or another platform rather than through the normal USFans purchasing workflow.

Do I need a separate Forward Order for every tracking number?

Yes. USFans’ forwarding agreement says each tracking number requires a separate order. If a seller splits a purchase into two shipments, register two records.

Can I send a cash-on-delivery parcel to the warehouse?

No. USFans states that only prepaid-freight parcels are supported; cash-on-delivery shipments will be refused and returned to the seller.

Why should I keep the tracking number private?

USFans identifies the domestic tracking number as the key proof that a forwarded parcel belongs to your account. Sharing it can create a parcel-ownership dispute.

How soon should forwarding logistics information be submitted?

USFans’ forwarding agreement says logistics information should be submitted within 15 days. Create the record before dispatch whenever possible instead of waiting for delivery.

What happens if the outer package is severely damaged?

The official receiving rules say a parcel that is severely damaged or fails acceptance standards may be refused or returned directly.

Does the warehouse open forwarded parcels?

Yes. USFans says accepted forwarding parcels are opened for prohibited-item screening before weighing and storage.

What if the parcel is delivered but the account does not show Received?

USFans notes that its warehouse is in Xiamen and gives three to five days as a typical domestic delivery reference. After a reasonable transit period, check the carrier proof and contact the carrier or seller before escalating with the complete record.

Does free forwarding QC verify the exact color, size and quantity?

The forwarding agreement says its free QC does not guarantee exact quantity, color or size verification and excludes several product-performance checks. Buyers should preserve seller evidence and request clarification when the visible record is insufficient.

How long are abnormal forwarded parcels stored?

USFans’ forwarding agreement states that abnormal parcels are stored for 60 days, so an exception alert should be handled promptly rather than left unresolved.

Make every physical parcel traceable

A successful forwarding order has one clear chain: seller order, dedicated address, prepaid domestic shipment, private tracking number, truthful product record, warehouse intake and stored item. Build that chain before delivery. When each physical package has its own evidence and every exception is handled quickly, self-purchased goods can enter the same international parcel-planning workflow without becoming unidentified inventory or forcing avoidable returns.
