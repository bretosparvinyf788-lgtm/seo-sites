08 / ANSWERSNO SALES SCRIPT

Ten clear answers

Independent answers about product discovery, QC, consolidation and shipping.

01What is a Sugargoo spreadsheet?+

An independent discovery index that organizes product leads into searchable categories. Always confirm the current source details before ordering.

02Is SugargooVIP an official Sugargoo website?+

No. SugargooVIP is independent and is not owned by or affiliated with Sugargoo.

03How do I use a product link?+

Open a find, review its source details, then use the marketplace URL with your chosen purchasing agent.

04Does Sugargoo provide QC photos?+

Current official guidance says warehouse inspection includes five free product photos per item; live conditions can change.

05Can QC photos guarantee quality?+

No. Photos help reveal visible details, but cannot guarantee authenticity, material or every hidden defect.

06How is international shipping calculated?+

It depends on destination, route, actual or volumetric weight, dimensions, restrictions and packaging.

07Should I remove shoe boxes?+

Only when saved volume matters more than protection or presentation. Decide item by item.

08Can I combine products from different sellers?+

Eligible items can generally be consolidated after warehouse arrival, subject to route restrictions.

09How often are links reviewed?+

High-traffic listings are prioritized, but price and availability may change after review.

10Does a listing prove authenticity?+

No. Inclusion is not authentication or endorsement. Follow all relevant laws and customs rules.
