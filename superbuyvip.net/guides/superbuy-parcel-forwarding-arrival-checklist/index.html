Superbuy Parcel Forwarding: Build an Arrival-Control Spreadsheet for Self-Purchased Orders

Parcel forwarding gives you control over the purchase, but it also makes you responsible for the handoff. This guide creates a disciplined record from seller dispatch to warehouse intake, so one missing identifier or vague carton description does not turn a cheap domestic order into an expensive search.

Forwarding changes who controls the purchase

Superbuy describes forwarding as separate from its shopping-agent service. With forwarding, you have already bought or prepared the goods and direct the seller to a Superbuy warehouse. This moves several controls onto you: the address, submitted item information, domestic tracking and the description used to match the arrival.

The main risk is identity loss. A seller may split one purchase, reuse an old address, omit your warehouse identifier or provide tracking for only part of the order. A clean sheet keeps the request, dispatch and warehouse receipt connected.

Create one row for every physical carton

The unit of control is the carton a carrier scans. Give each carton an internal reference and record the forwarding request, seller, marketplace order, items, quantities, warehouse, address snapshot, user identifier, carrier, tracking number, expected dispatch and status.

Add the review result, latest acceptable arrival, warehouse match and route status. If a seller splits the shipment, duplicate the row, add a carton suffix and assign the second tracking number. Never place two active numbers in one cell.

Treat the warehouse address as controlled data

Superbuy's forwarding guide says a unique identification number appears at the end of each user's address and can be found in the account's shipping-address area. It also directs users to obtain current warehouse addresses through the Parcel Forwarding section. This means the address is not a generic text block copied from an old forum post. It is account-specific operational data.

Copy every line from the live account address and save a dated screenshot. Compare the seller's entry with it, including identifier and warehouse. Do not shorten unfamiliar text or merge fields without confirming the result. Resolve form problems before dispatch; a tracking number cannot repair an incomplete address.

Submit the forwarding request before dispatch

The official workflow begins with item information review. Superbuy says the submitted information is normally reviewed within one business day and the result is delivered through an inbox message. Only after approval should the parcel be prepared and sent. Use that review as a gate: the seller does not receive permission to dispatch until the sheet contains an approved request and a verified destination.

The current guide instructs users to send the parcel within 15 business days and complete logistics information before expiry. Record the request, review, internal ship-by date and expiry separately. Set your deadline earlier because seller handling and domestic transit consume the window.

Make the logistics record prove movement

A seller's “shipped” button is not enough. Copy the carrier and tracking number into the Superbuy request and your sheet, then look for a carrier acceptance scan. Superbuy notes that the forwarding order changes to Shipped after logistics information is completed. That status proves data entry, not warehouse arrival.

Use a short status sequence: approved, seller released, carrier accepted, in transit, delivered scan, warehouse matched and stocked. A delivered scan without a stocked record becomes an exception row, not a completed row. First verify the carrier, destination and delivery time; then send a concise enquiry containing the forwarding request, tracking number, carton description and delivery evidence.

Reconcile what arrived, not what you hoped arrived

Superbuy says a received item is weighed, inspected and shown in My Warehouse. Its forwarding page also warns that only the item number and name are confirmed while mentioning inspection and three photos. Use the record as visible intake evidence, not proof of authenticity, internal function, exact material or concealed quantity.

Compare five facts immediately: forwarding reference, item name, expected quantity, recorded weight and visible outer condition. Then ask whether the available images answer your prewritten acceptance questions. A sealed electronic device may look correct while remaining untested. A bundle photograph may not prove the count beneath the top layer. If an unanswered question could change your decision, request an available targeted service or contact support before consolidation.

Separate arrival evidence from seller remedies

Because you made the purchase, preserve the seller's return terms and buyer-protection deadline. The warehouse receipt and seller remedy deadlines are different clocks. Superbuy's 90 days of free storage does not extend a marketplace return window.

If the arrival is wrong, build a factual comparison: what was ordered, what the seller said was dispatched, what the carrier delivered and what the warehouse evidence shows. Avoid demanding a conclusion that the photos cannot support. Ask for a specific observable detail, then use that result in the marketplace claim or seller negotiation. Mark the row resolved only when the replacement, return, refund or decision to keep is documented.

Screen route eligibility before consolidation

Acceptance into a warehouse does not guarantee that every international route can carry the item. Superbuy says users are responsible for knowing prohibited international items, while route choices can be restricted by item type. Record batteries, liquids, powders, magnets, food, fragile construction and unusual dimensions as review flags, not as labels you invent to avoid a restriction.

After stocking, check routes offered for the actual destination and goods. If one product reduces the eligible lines, compare separate shipment with consolidation. Keep declarations accurate; the sheet should expose a restriction, never disguise one.

Close the loop after international submission

Superbuy's published process moves stocked items through parcel submission, a shipping-fee deposit, packing, final weight verification and delivery tracking. Link each forwarding-carton row to the eventual international parcel number. Record which goods were consolidated, packing services requested, the route selected and the final freight adjustment. That creates an audit trail from the original seller to the parcel at your door.

After delivery, compare received contents with the intake records before closing the sheet. Note any missing or damaged item while labels and packaging are still available. The best forwarding log is not a warehouse diary; it is a chain of custody that explains where responsibility changed and what evidence existed at every handoff.

Frequently asked questions

1. Is Superbuy parcel forwarding the same as the shopping-agent service?

No. In a forwarding order, you purchase or prepare the goods and arrange domestic delivery to the warehouse. The shopping-agent service places the purchase for you. That makes address accuracy, seller coordination and domestic tracking especially important for forwarding.

2. When should I create the forwarding request?

Create it before the seller dispatches. Submit an accurate item name and type, wait for review, confirm the selected warehouse and copy the live account-specific address. Releasing the parcel first removes the chance to correct a rejected item or destination.

3. How long does the information review take?

Superbuy's current forwarding guide says the submitted item information is reviewed within one business day and the result is sent through an inbox message. Treat that as the published workflow, while leaving extra time for questions or incomplete descriptions.

4. Why is the unique address identifier important?

It helps the warehouse associate an arrival with the correct user under special circumstances. Copy the entire current address from your account, including the identifier at the end, and verify the seller's saved destination before dispatch.

5. What is the 15-business-day rule?

The official guide instructs users to send the parcel to the selected warehouse within 15 business days and complete logistics information before expiry. Record an earlier internal deadline so seller handling and domestic transit do not consume the whole window.

6. What if one marketplace order is split into two cartons?

Create a separate spreadsheet row for each physical carton and tracking number. Retain the shared marketplace order reference, but give each carton its own contents, quantity, movement status and warehouse match. This makes a partial arrival visible.

7. Does the Shipped status mean the warehouse received my parcel?

No. Superbuy says that status appears after logistics information is completed. Confirm a carrier delivery scan and then wait for the item to be matched and displayed in My Warehouse before treating the domestic handoff as complete.

8. Does forwarding inspection prove product quality or authenticity?

No. Treat intake information and photos as evidence of visible identity and condition only. They may not prove authenticity, internal operation, materials or hidden contents. Ask a targeted question or service only when its answer can change your decision.

9. What should I do if tracking says delivered but nothing appears in My Warehouse?

Verify the carrier, destination and delivery timestamp, then prepare the request number, tracking number, carton description, address identifier and delivery evidence. Send one concise enquiry and keep the row open until the parcel is matched or the discrepancy is explained.

10. Does 90-day free storage extend my seller's return period?

No. Warehouse storage and the marketplace remedy window are independent. Record the seller or platform deadline before dispatch, review arrival evidence promptly and start any return or dispute while that external option is still available.

11. Can every stocked forwarding item use every shipping line?

No. Eligibility depends on item characteristics, destination and route rules. Record restriction flags honestly, inspect the routes displayed for the actual goods and compare separate shipment when one item makes the entire parcel harder to route.

12. When is a forwarding row complete?

Close it only after the warehouse match is verified, discrepancies are resolved, the item is linked to an international parcel and the delivered result is recorded. A domestic delivered scan or a warehouse photo alone is only one handoff in the chain.

Working rule: one carton, one tracking number, one row. Every status must point to evidence, and every unresolved difference must have an owner and a deadline.
