WAREHOUSE EVIDENCE / DECISION CONTROL

QC is evidence, not decoration.

Warehouse photos are most useful when you compare them with the order you actually placed. A strong LitBuy QC workflow checks identity first, then visible condition and details, and finishes with one clear action: approve, clarify, exchange or return.

Separate what a photo proves from what it cannot prove

A photo can show visible color, shape, labeling, surface condition and some construction details. It may help confirm that the received item resembles the variant you ordered. It cannot automatically prove long-term durability, exact material composition, internal construction or how the item will fit you.

That distinction keeps QC useful. Instead of asking whether a photo “looks good,” ask whether it resolves the specific risk that matters for your order.

APPROVE

Evidence matches

The visible item and selected variant are consistent with the order, with no issue important enough to block shipment.

CLARIFY

Evidence is incomplete

You cannot see the detail needed to decide. Identify the exact area or measurement that needs clarification.

EXCHANGE

Correctable mismatch

A wrong variant or visible defect may justify an exchange when the seller and order conditions allow it.

RETURN

Do not keep it

The received item does not meet the purchase decision and you do not want it included in the final parcel.

REPEATABLE REVIEW

A five-pass QC sequence

Confirm item identity

Match the warehouse item to the correct order before judging details. Similar-looking products can be confused when several purchases arrive close together.

Check the selected variant

Compare visible size, color, model or other variant information with the order record. A beautiful photo of the wrong variant is still a failed order.

Scan for visible condition issues

Look for damage, obvious marks, asymmetry, missing parts or packaging problems that matter for the specific product.

Inspect the high-risk detail

Focus on the feature that would change your decision: dimensions, hardware, print placement, stitching area, accessory count or another relevant point.

Record one decision

Finish the review with an action. Leaving an item in an unresolved state is what creates warehouse delays and messy parcel planning later.

FAQ

LitBuy QC questions

Should I judge QC from one detail only?

Start with order identity and variant accuracy, then evaluate the visible issue that matters most. A single cosmetic detail should not distract you from a wrong size, wrong color or wrong item.

What if the photos do not show the area I need?

Do not invent certainty from missing evidence. Treat the item as unresolved until you have enough information to make the approve, exchange or return decision.

Can QC photos prove product quality?

They can document visible characteristics of the received unit, but they cannot prove every property of a product. Use them as warehouse evidence, not as a universal quality guarantee.

Why review QC before parcel planning?

Because parcel decisions should be based on items you actually intend to keep. Unresolved exchanges or returns can change weight, timing and consolidation choices.
