What this LitBuy Spreadsheet hub is for

The goal is not to treat every row as an automatic purchase recommendation. Marketplace conditions change: listings move, seller inventory changes, variants can differ, and a product that looked useful when it was added may need to be checked again before ordering.

A stronger workflow separates discovery from verification. The spreadsheet helps you shortlist. The product page tells you what is currently offered. Warehouse photos and order details provide evidence after the item arrives. Parcel planning happens only after you understand which items are worth keeping.

COMMON QUESTIONS

LitBuy Spreadsheet FAQ

Is the spreadsheet the same as a live marketplace?

No. Treat a spreadsheet as a discovery and organization layer. Always re-check the live listing before ordering because seller inventory, variants and pricing can change.

What should I verify before buying from a spreadsheet row?

Confirm that the destination listing is live, the intended variant is selectable, the current product details match your expectations, and the order information you submit is correct.

Why should QC come after product discovery?

Discovery tells you what might be worth buying. QC gives you evidence about the specific unit that actually reached the warehouse. Those are different decisions and should not be collapsed into one.

Should I submit a parcel as soon as the first item arrives?

Usually it is more useful to review the status of the full group you intend to consolidate. Staggered arrivals, exchanges and unresolved QC questions can affect the final parcel plan.
