Understand the two-stage Superbuy workflow

Superbuy acts as a shopping and shipping intermediary for products sold through Chinese marketplaces. The official workflow has two broad stages. First, you submit a product, pay for the item and Chinese domestic delivery, and Superbuy's agent purchases it from the seller. The seller sends it to Superbuy's warehouse, where the item is stored and visible inspection evidence is provided. Second, you select warehouse items, choose an international route and packing services, pay a shipping deposit and track the parcel to your address. Keeping the stages separate prevents the most common beginner mistake: assuming the first payment includes international delivery. It also creates two decision gates—before the agent buys and after the warehouse receives—where you can stop, clarify or change course.

Create the order from a live marketplace link

Start with the current Taobao, Tmall, JD, 1688 or other supported marketplace page, not an old screenshot or a copied title. Superbuy's official guide says you can paste a product link into its search bar or search by product name. For many Taobao, Tmall and JD products, Superbuy captures product details automatically. If the system cannot parse the listing, it opens a manual shopping-agent order where you enter the product information. In either case, compare the imported price, seller, variation and domestic freight with the live source page. Marketplace listings can change or reuse URLs. Save the exact title, selected option, price and images before submitting so you can later tell whether a seller change or a selection error caused a mismatch.

Translate the specification into an order fingerprint

Color and size labels are not enough when a listing contains many similar options. Write an order fingerprint with the Chinese variation text, an English explanation, quantity, measurements, model number and one or two reference images. Add only essential remarks that the agent can act on. For clothing, compare numerical measurements with an item you own; do not trust S, M or L across sellers. For 1688, check minimum order quantity, tiered pricing and whether one listing combines multiple products. For Weidian or manually entered orders, verify seller identity and domestic delivery. The fingerprint is your acceptance standard at the warehouse. If you cannot explain exactly which variation should arrive, warehouse photos will not solve the ambiguity later.

Review price, domestic freight and seller terms before payment

The first Superbuy payment normally covers merchandise and Chinese local delivery shown for the order, not the later international parcel. Confirm the unit price, quantity, domestic shipping and any seller-imposed minimum before paying. Superbuy's official tutorial lists multiple payment methods and instructs buyers to add destination information, remarks and selected value-added services during review. Payment availability and processing costs can vary by method, currency, account and date, so rely on the live checkout rather than a fixed fee copied from an old guide. Save the order total and currency. If a price changes after submission, decide from your pre-set maximum instead of automatically accepting because time has already been invested.

Use order enquiry for questions the listing cannot answer

During purchasing, Superbuy says buyers can use order enquiry to contact the shopping agent, and the agent may send an email or message when information is missing. Ask decision-ready questions: confirm whether the seller accepts returns, whether a bonus is included, whether a 1688 minimum can mix colors, or whether the dispatch date is still valid. Include the order number and the action you want if the answer is negative. Avoid sending several unrelated questions in one vague message. The agent can relay information, but no intermediary can make an unclear listing certain. Save the response next to the original order fingerprint because the answer may become important when the item reaches the warehouse.

Wait for warehouse storage and inspect systematically

The seller ships domestically to Superbuy, and the order status changes as it is purchased, dispatched, received and stored. Superbuy's official guidance says warehouse staff perform quality inspection and take photos for you to review in My Warehouse. It also states that items receive 90 days of free storage under the current guidance, which allows time to consolidate compatible purchases. Review warehouse evidence in a fixed sequence: identity and quantity, selected variation, visible measurements or labels, construction, obvious damage and shipping-sensitive features. Photos cannot prove durability, authenticity, internal electronics, exact material composition or hidden contents. If a visible question would change your decision, request clarification before international parcel submission.

Decide whether to accept, exchange or return while options remain

Write one outcome for each warehouse item: accept, ask, exchange or return. Compare the photos with the saved listing and order fingerprint rather than with memory. If the seller sent the wrong option or a visible defect, open the relevant order and ask promptly about the available after-sales process. Seller rules, marketplace timing and product category can limit returns, especially for custom, pre-sale or some 1688 purchases. Superbuy facilitates communication but does not erase seller terms. Do not ship internationally just because a defect looks minor in one image. Once the parcel leaves China, correction becomes slower and often more expensive. The warehouse decision gate is where a disciplined buyer protects the rest of the budget.

Consolidate only items that belong on the same route

When accepted items are ready, select them in My Warehouse and submit a parcel. Superbuy's guide says buyers can combine stored items to save shipping fees, but one giant parcel is not automatically best. Batteries, liquids, powders, food, branded goods, fragile products and oversized packaging may reduce route eligibility or demand different handling. Group items by compatibility, urgency, value concentration and protection needs. Decide whether retail packaging should remain, be folded or be removed. Add reinforcement, waterproofing or insurance only after reading the live terms and considering the actual risk. Save the final included-item list; it will later be the baseline for delivery and missing-item checks.

Compare routes and pay the international shipping deposit

Enter the delivery address carefully, then compare the recommended routes available for the real parcel. Superbuy's official calculator uses destination, weight, dimensions and item category, and its tutorial says the deposit is based on estimated weight, method and destination. Review speed, tracking, restrictions, size limits and compensation terms beside price. The carrier verifies the packed parcel later, so final freight can differ from the deposit; Superbuy reconciles the difference after shipment. Customs taxes and destination charges are separate and depend on local rules. Preserve the submitted address, route, packing instructions, estimated weight, deposit and declaration information. These records make later tracking or after-sales questions much easier to answer.

Track delivery and close the order with evidence

After dispatch, Superbuy directs buyers to My Superbuy and Parcels for tracking. Save the parcel number and carrier number. When delivery arrives, photograph the unopened carton and label, record any damage, and make a continuous unboxing video for valuable or complex parcels. Count items against the warehouse and parcel lists before discarding packaging. If something is missing, damaged or incorrect, open the relevant after-sales path with a compact evidence packet rather than a general complaint. Finally, record actual freight, delivery time, item condition and seller accuracy in your spreadsheet. A good Superbuy tutorial ends after delivery, not after checkout, because the completed record is what improves the next Taobao, 1688 or Weidian purchase.

How to Use Superbuy: 12 Practical Questions

What does Superbuy do?

Superbuy purchases supported Chinese marketplace goods for users, receives them at its warehouse, provides visible inspection evidence and offers international parcel delivery options.

Can I paste a Taobao product link into Superbuy?

Yes. Superbuy's official tutorial says buyers can paste a Taobao, Tmall or JD link, and many listings are captured automatically.

What if Superbuy cannot read a product page?

Complete the manual shopping-agent order with the seller, item link, variation, quantity, price, domestic freight and clear remarks.

Can I use Superbuy for 1688?

Yes, but check minimum quantities, tier pricing, variation rules and the specific inspection or after-sales terms shown for the order.

Can I use Superbuy for Weidian?

Supported links can be submitted; if details are not captured, verify the seller and enter the order manually with a precise product fingerprint.

Does the first payment include international shipping?

Usually no. The purchase stage covers merchandise and Chinese domestic delivery; international freight is selected and paid after warehouse arrival.

What are Superbuy warehouse photos for?

They help verify visible identity, variation, quantity and damage. They cannot prove authenticity, durability, hidden contents or internal performance.

How long is free Superbuy storage?

Superbuy's current official guidance states 90 days of free storage for purchased items. Confirm the live terms in your account before relying on the full period.

Should I combine all warehouse items into one parcel?

Not automatically. Group items by route compatibility, restrictions, urgency, value, fragility and packaging needs before deciding.

Why is international shipping paid as a deposit?

The parcel is not finally measured until packing and carrier verification. Superbuy reconciles the estimate with final freight after shipment.

Where do I track a Superbuy parcel?

Superbuy's tutorial directs buyers to My Superbuy and the Parcels area for tracking after dispatch.

What should I save after delivery?

Keep the parcel record, label, unboxing evidence, final freight, delivery time, item condition and any after-sales case for future buying decisions.
