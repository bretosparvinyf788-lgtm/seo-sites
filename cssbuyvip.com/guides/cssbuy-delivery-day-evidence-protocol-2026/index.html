CSSBuy Delivery-Day Evidence Protocol: Inspect, Record and Resolve Parcel Problems

The first minutes after delivery can determine whether a missing-item or damage enquiry is clear or impossible to reconstruct. This protocol turns the unopened carton, opening sequence and item count into one reliable record.

Published August 3, 2026 · Slug: cssbuy-delivery-day-evidence-protocol-2026

The parcel is not finished when the tracking page says delivered

Most buyers spend hours comparing W2C links, checking warehouse photos and estimating international shipping, then treat delivery as the easy part. That is backwards. The first minutes after a CSSBuy parcel reaches your door are the only time when the carton, seals, labels, weight and contents can be documented in the condition in which they arrived. Once the box is opened, packaging is thrown away or items are mixed with your existing wardrobe, a later parcel enquiry becomes much harder to explain.

A delivery-day evidence protocol is not a promise that every problem will be reimbursed. It is a method for preserving facts. CSSBuy’s current process separates order questions from parcel questions: purchase, shipment verification, returns and refunds are handled through the relevant order’s Add Note, while tracking, logistics and parcel-detail questions are handled through My Parcel. The protocol below helps you collect the information needed before choosing the correct channel.

Prepare a five-minute receiving station

Do not open the parcel on the doorstep unless local delivery rules require it. Move it to a clean, well-lit surface and prepare a phone with enough storage, a scale if available, a measuring tape, scissors and the order list. A plain table is better than a bed or carpet because small accessories, tags and hardware are easier to see.

Start with four exterior photographs: the top, bottom, two long sides and both short sides. Photograph the shipping label, tracking number, visible carrier stickers, crushed corners, wet areas, cuts, retaping or punctures. Keep private address information out of anything you plan to share publicly, but retain an uncropped private copy for support if it is needed.

Before opening

Record all sides, labels, seals and visible damage while the package is still closed.

During opening

Use one continuous video that shows the sealed parcel, opening process and first view of the contents.

After opening

Reconcile every item against the warehouse and parcel records before discarding packaging.

Record the closed parcel before changing it

If the parcel looks damaged, do not repair the box before documenting it. Tape, dents and missing labels can help distinguish warehouse packaging from damage that occurred later. Record the parcel’s actual weight and three dimensions when possible. These numbers are not a substitute for the carrier’s measurements, but they create a useful receiving record when the package appears unusually light, compressed or opened.

CSSBuy’s estimator shows that shipping lines can use actual weight, volumetric weight, size restrictions and route-specific limits. It also displays a maximum insured value for available methods when that data is provided. Because route rules differ, save the shipping-method name, parcel number, declared content summary and any insurance selection from your account before the page or route data changes. Do not assume that an insurance option covers every event or every amount; preserve the exact choice shown for your parcel.

Evidence rule: the strongest record connects one parcel number to one unopened carton, one opening sequence and one item-by-item count.

Use a continuous opening video correctly

A useful unboxing video is simple, not cinematic. Begin with the tracking label and every sealed edge in frame. Show the parcel number, then open the box without pausing the recording. Avoid moving the camera away while another person removes items. If an item is missing, a continuous sequence is more persuasive than separate clips filmed after the contents have been rearranged.

Lift out packing material slowly. Show empty corners and the bottom of the carton before ending the video. For multiple bags or inner boxes, open them in the same recording when practical, especially if the outer package arrived damaged or the parcel contains small high-value pieces. If the video becomes too long, narrate the count clearly: “parcel one, inner bag two, item three.”

Do not destroy evidence to create a dramatic shot. Cut away from the product, keep labels attached and avoid tearing inner packaging until its condition is recorded. If liquid has leaked or glass is broken, protect yourself first. Photograph the hazard from a safe distance and isolate it before continuing.

Run a three-way reconciliation

After opening, compare three records: what you ordered, what CSSBuy showed in the warehouse and what arrived in the parcel. These are different checkpoints. The seller may have sent the wrong variant; the warehouse may have photographed the correct item but a parcel may contain the wrong quantity; or the carton may show signs of loss during transport.

Create a short discrepancy list. Use one line per issue: “Order 123 — black size M expected, blue size L received,” or “Parcel item list shows two belts, one belt present.” Avoid a long emotional message before the facts are organized. Support teams can act faster when each claim is linked to an order number, parcel number and clear photo.

Classify the problem before contacting support

Not every disappointing item is a parcel problem. A loose thread, poor stitching or inaccurate seller sizing is usually a product-quality issue. A crushed retail box may be packaging or carrier damage. A missing item could be a warehouse packing discrepancy, a split shipment misunderstanding or transport loss. Classification matters because CSSBuy directs order enquiries and parcel enquiries to different places.

Use My Order when the issue concerns the purchase, SKU, seller communication, warehouse inspection, return or refund. Use My Parcel when the issue concerns tracking, logistics, parcel details, delivery damage or missing contents after international shipment. If both stages may be involved, open the parcel enquiry first with the parcel evidence, then reference the affected order numbers without duplicating contradictory messages across many tickets.

CSSBuy’s current Buy For Me guidance also states that third-party logistics and customs can involve delays, taxes, confiscation, damage or missing parcels, and that those external outcomes cannot be guaranteed. That makes precise evidence more important, not less. A clear record helps separate an event that happened before dispatch from one that may have happened after handoff.

Keep packaging until the case is understood

Do not discard the outer carton, shipping label, inner bags, protective materials or damaged product packaging immediately. Keep them together until you know that every item is present and functional. Carrier labels and weight stickers can be especially useful. Photograph serial numbers, size labels and product codes before removing tags.

For electronics, test basic function without modifying the item. Do not install custom software, remove safety seals or dismantle the product before recording the original condition. For clothing, verify size labels and measurements before washing. For shoes and bags, inspect pairs, hardware, straps and removable accessories. For fragile goods, photograph the protective layers and the exact location of breakage.

If there is no problem, the evidence can be deleted after a reasonable period. The process still has value: it confirms that the parcel list, warehouse record and delivered contents match.

Write an evidence-first parcel note

A strong Add Note message has five parts: parcel number, delivery date, problem type, affected order numbers and attached evidence. State what you expected and what you found. Mention whether the outer carton was damaged, retaped or dry and intact. List the files in a logical order: exterior photos, label, opening video, item count and close-up evidence.

Practical format:
Parcel: [number]
Delivered: [date and local time]
Issue: [missing item / visible damage / wrong parcel content]
Affected orders: [numbers]
Evidence: closed-carton photos, label photo, continuous opening video and item-count photos.

Do not exaggerate. If you are unsure whether an item was omitted or packed inside another product box, say that you completed a full carton check and show the empty packaging. If a parcel was left unattended, state that fact. Accurate uncertainty is more useful than a confident guess.

Build the evidence plan before shipping

The best delivery protocol begins before the parcel leaves China. Save the final parcel item list, route name, estimated and charged amounts, packaging choices and insurance selection. Confirm that small accessories are listed and that items with similar appearance can be identified. If several identical products are included, ask whether quantity is visible in the warehouse record.

Use focused QC requests for serial labels, model numbers, sizes or accessory counts that will matter at delivery. This creates a before-and-after comparison. A general photo of five folded garments is weak evidence for quantity; a photo showing five separate labels is much stronger.

Finally, tell anyone receiving the parcel not to open it casually. A family member who tears away the label and mixes the contents can unintentionally erase the cleanest evidence. Share the protocol before delivery day.

The delivery-day decision sequence

  1. Match the tracking number to the expected CSSBuy parcel.
  2. Photograph the unopened carton, label, seals and visible damage.
  3. Record weight and dimensions when practical.
  4. Film one continuous opening sequence.
  5. Compare delivered contents with the parcel list and warehouse evidence.
  6. Keep all packaging and labels until the count and condition are confirmed.
  7. Classify the issue as order-stage, warehouse-stage or parcel-stage.
  8. Use the relevant Add Note channel with a concise evidence index.

This sequence turns delivery from an informal unboxing into a controlled final checkpoint. It does not make international shipping risk-free, but it gives you a reliable record of what arrived, how it arrived and which CSSBuy workflow should handle the question.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I record every CSSBuy parcel opening?

It is especially useful for valuable, fragile, complex or visibly damaged parcels. A short continuous recording creates a clean receiving record.

What should appear at the start of the video?

Show the tracking label, parcel number, all sealed edges and the unopened condition of the carton before cutting it.

Where should I report a missing item after international delivery?

Start with the relevant parcel in My Parcel and use Add Note, then identify every affected order number in the message.

Where should I report a wrong size or seller variant?

Use the affected order in My Order and Add Note because the issue concerns the purchase, SKU or warehouse-stage record.

Should I throw away the carton after filming?

No. Keep the carton, shipping label, inner packaging and damaged materials until the contents and condition are fully resolved.

Does an insurance option guarantee reimbursement?

No. Coverage and limits can vary by shipping method and event. Preserve the exact option and maximum insured value shown for your parcel.

What if the carrier left the parcel unattended?

Document where it was found, the exterior condition and the time, then state those facts accurately in the parcel enquiry.

Do I need a scale?

A scale is helpful when the parcel seems unusually light or damaged, but photos, labels, a continuous opening video and item reconciliation are the core evidence.

How should I report several problems in one parcel?

Use one numbered line per issue and connect each issue to the relevant order number, photo or video timestamp.

Should I wash clothing before checking it?

No. Confirm size labels, measurements, quantity and visible condition before washing or removing tags.

What if I cannot tell whether the problem happened at the warehouse or in transit?

Open a parcel enquiry with the complete evidence and reference the affected orders. Describe what is known without guessing.

What records should I save before CSSBuy ships the parcel?

Save the item list, route name, parcel number, packaging choices, chargeable-weight information, insurance selection and key warehouse photos.
