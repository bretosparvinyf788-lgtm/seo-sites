BUYER DESK

Questions worth asking before you pay

Clear answers about links, QC, shipping and the limits of an independent spreadsheet.

10 detailed answers
01Is SugargooVIP an official Sugargoo website?＋

No. SugargooVIP is an independent discovery and education site. Ordering, payment, warehouse activity, shipping, refunds and account support take place on Sugargoo or the relevant marketplace.

02What is a Sugargoo spreadsheet used for?＋

It is a faster way to discover and compare product links from Taobao, Weidian and 1688. The live seller page and your own warehouse QC remain the final checkpoints.

03Are the prices on product cards final?＋

No. Prices are discovery references. Sellers can change price, domestic freight, variants or stock. International shipping and services are separate.

04How do I order a product I find here?＋

Open the product card, verify the listing, copy the complete URL and paste it into Sugargoo. Confirm the variation and inspect warehouse QC before shipping.

05Do spreadsheet photos replace warehouse QC?＋

No. Catalogue images identify a product; warehouse QC shows the item connected to your order. QC still cannot prove hidden defects or long-term durability.

06What should I check in Sugargoo QC photos?＋

Confirm model, size, colour and quantity first. Then check the details specific to shoes, clothing, bags or electronics.

07Why can shipping cost more than the item?＋

International cost depends on actual or dimensional weight, packaging, destination, product attributes and available routes.

08Can every product be shipped to every country?＋

No. Carrier rules and product attributes matter. Liquids, batteries, magnets, electronics and some branded goods can have fewer routes.

09How often should product links be checked?＋

Every link should be checked immediately before ordering because marketplace listings can change or disappear without notice.

10Does a low price mean the product is good value?＋

Not necessarily. Compare the variation, seller information, measurements, QC risk and likely shipping cost before deciding.
