CSSBuy Expert Buy Guide: How to Submit a Manual Product Order

Build a manual purchasing request that an agent can execute without guessing the product, variant, quantity, domestic freight or seller terms.

01 · ROUTE SELECTION

Choose Expert Buy for the right problem

Expert Buy is not a shortcut around careful product research. It is a structured fallback when the ordinary CSSBuy search or link parser cannot create a usable product page. CSSBuy's Buy For Me instructions direct buyers to Expert Buy when an item cannot be searched, and the official form is designed to accept product information manually. That makes it useful for sources such as Yupoo or seller-led orders where the visible page does not behave like a conventional marketplace listing.

Begin with a route test. If the standard link imports a complete title, selectable variant, quantity and price, use the ordinary flow because those fields remain attached to the source record. Move to Expert Buy when the importer fails, the page exposes only a catalogue image, or the seller has supplied ordering details outside the listing.

THE FALLBACK TEST

Use Expert Buy only when you can identify the exact seller, exact item and exact terms more clearly than the automated page can. A broken parser is solvable; an unknown seller or undefined version is not.

02 · SOURCE IDENTITY

Create a source identity record

The official Expert Buy form asks for an item URL and item title. Treat these as two independent identifiers. The URL shows where the request came from; the title tells the purchasing agent what the link is supposed to represent. A title such as "black jacket" is too weak. A useful title combines product type, seller model name or code, chosen color, size and any edition marker visible in the seller's material.

Save the seller's contact identity and the date on which the price was confirmed. For a catalogue source, capture the specific album page rather than a general storefront when possible. If the seller gave instructions through a message, preserve the complete exchange that confirms price, domestic shipping, variant and availability. Do not assume that a product image proves who will receive payment.

  • Record the most specific available product page or seller reference.
  • Give the item a title that distinguishes it from nearby versions.
  • Save the quoted terms before the seller changes or removes them.
  • Keep seller identity separate from a reseller's promotional image.
03 · VISUAL SPECIFICATION

Turn pictures into version evidence

Expert Buy accepts images and a note. Use images to identify, not merely decorate, the request. Start with one full product view, then add only the pictures needed to distinguish the requested version: a color swatch, label, closure, sole pattern, accessory set or model code. When several variants appear in one collage, mark the chosen one before uploading. An unmarked collage asks the agent to guess.

Visual similarity is especially dangerous when sellers reuse catalogue photographs across different batches. Pair every picture with a written constraint. "The version with the silver buckle shown in image two" is actionable; "same as photo" is not, because a photo may contain several details.

04 · COMMERCIAL FIELDS

Separate every commercial field

The Expert Buy form separates price in RMB, domestic shipping and quantity. Keep them separate in your evidence sheet too. The item price should be the seller's unit or lot price under the confirmed quantity rule. Domestic shipping is the seller's charge for moving the order to the CSSBuy warehouse, not international delivery. Quantity is the number of units the agent should purchase, not the number of pictures or colors displayed.

Before submission, rebuild the expected goods total yourself: unit price multiplied by quantity, plus the quoted domestic shipping. This is an arithmetic check, not a promise of the final international cost. CSSBuy's broader Buy For Me workflow treats the goods and Chinese local delivery as the purchasing stage; international shipping is handled later after warehouse intake and parcel submission. Keeping those stages apart prevents a low domestic quote from being mistaken for the cost of delivery to your country.

05 · EXECUTABLE NOTE

Write a constraint-based note

A good note has three layers: selection, rejection and confirmation. Selection states the exact size, color, version, quantity and included parts. Rejection names the substitutions you will not accept, such as a different shade or an alternative model. Confirmation tells the agent when to pause, for example if the seller changes price, shipping charge, stock status or return terms.

Keep the language short enough to translate cleanly. Use one decision per line and copy seller option names exactly where available. Avoid life stories, jokes and vague quality requests. CSSBuy can contact the seller during purchasing, but the agent needs a specific question. "Confirm whether the detachable strap is included before payment" can be answered. "Please make sure everything is perfect" cannot.

  1. Order identity: product, model and seller reference.
  2. Variant: seller color name, size and version marker.
  3. Quantity: total units and allocation if more than one variant.
  4. Inclusions: accessories or components that must be present.
  5. Stop rule: changes that require your approval before payment.
06 · SELLER RISK

Price the protection gap before you order

CSSBuy states that products handled through its shopping-agent service come from third-party platforms and are not sold directly by CSSBuy. Its Expert Buy guidance gives an additional warning for sellers on WeChat, Yupoo or Weidian: those transactions may lack buyer protection, and CSSBuy may be unable to charge back if a seller does not ship or refuses a return. This is not a small disclaimer hidden after checkout; it changes the decision standard before payment.

Ask whether the seller has a stable history, whether the exact item is available, and whether returns are accepted for the reason that matters to you. Reduce exposure on an unfamiliar source by avoiding unnecessary quantity and by refusing ambiguous substitutions. Do not treat warehouse QC as payment protection. QC can document what arrived; it cannot recover funds from a seller who sends nothing, nor can it create a return right the seller never offered.

RISK RULE

If the loss would be unacceptable without a chargeback or guaranteed return, the seller channel is not suitable for that order, regardless of how attractive the product looks.

07 · PURCHASE HANDOFF

Manage the purchasing handoff

After submission, the request becomes a purchasing conversation rather than a finished specification frozen in time. CSSBuy says its team contacts the seller and purchases the ordered items. During that stage, buyers can use Add Note for further details, and CSSBuy may contact them when information is incomplete. Watch the order status and respond to a concrete question with a concrete answer.

Keep order enquiries attached to the relevant order. CSSBuy's service page directs users to My Order, the relevant order and Add Note for purchase, shipment-verification and return questions. Do not scatter the same request across unrelated channels. A single chronological record makes it easier to see whether the seller changed price, whether you approved a substitution and what the purchasing agent was instructed to do.

08 · WAREHOUSE ACCEPTANCE

Audit the warehouse arrival against the request

The official Buy For Me flow places inspection and storage after purchase. When the item appears in the warehouse record, compare it with the manual request, not with your memory of the seller's gallery. Check identity first, then variant, quantity, visible condition and included parts. Use the saved pictures and note as your acceptance specification.

Separate observable evidence from performance claims. A warehouse photo may show a model label, seam, accessory or color family. It cannot prove long-term durability, material chemistry or authenticity. If a required detail is not visible, ask a narrow question before parcel submission. If the wrong version arrived, keep the item out of the international parcel while you investigate seller return options. Once several items are consolidated, a simple product dispute can become a parcel-level delay.

International delivery remains a later decision. CSSBuy's flow asks the buyer to select stored items, submit delivery details and choose from available or recommended methods, with some products restricted on certain routes. The manual order therefore passes two separate approvals: acceptance into your warehouse inventory and acceptance into an international parcel.

09 · PRACTICAL FAQ

Manual-order questions answered

Should I use Expert Buy whenever a product is cheaper elsewhere?

No. Use it when the ordinary search or import flow cannot represent a verifiable order. A lower price does not repair missing seller identity, unclear variants or weak protection.

What if I have a picture but no conventional product link?

The official Expert Buy guidance describes image-led requests for seller channels without a normal listing. You still need seller information, price, domestic shipping, quantity and an exact written specification. A picture alone is not an executable order.

Can CSSBuy decide the best size for me?

Do not delegate fit preference to the purchasing agent. Compare the seller's chart with your own garment or body measurements and submit the seller's exact size option. If a measurement is unclear, ask before purchase.

Is domestic shipping the same as international shipping?

No. Domestic shipping moves goods from the seller to the CSSBuy warehouse. International shipping is selected and paid later when stored items are submitted as a parcel.

What happens when the seller changes the price?

Your note should require approval before purchase when a quoted commercial field changes. Reply with the exact price and specification you accept so the record is unambiguous.

Does warehouse QC remove seller risk?

No. Warehouse evidence helps you assess goods that arrive. It does not guarantee that an unprotected seller will ship, accept a return or refund payment.

Where should I ask an order-specific question?

Keep it on the relevant order through My Order and Add Note. Parcel tracking or parcel-detail questions belong on the relevant record in My Parcel after a parcel exists.

10 · FINAL CONTROL

Use a two-key approval

Before submitting Expert Buy, require two keys. The specification key is complete when the source, item, images, variant, quantity, price, domestic shipping and stop rules agree. The risk key is complete when you understand the seller channel, protection limits and return uncertainty. If either key is missing, pause.

Repeat the same discipline at the warehouse. Approve the item only when the arrival matches the saved request closely enough for your purpose. Approve international shipment only when the item is suitable for the chosen parcel and available route. Expert Buy works best when manual does not mean informal: every field becomes evidence, every uncertainty has an owner, and every payment follows a defined decision.
