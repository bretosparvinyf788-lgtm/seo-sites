What the LitBuy spreadsheet contains

The live catalog contains 120 curated product records across Sneakers, Streetwear, Bags, Jackets, Accessories and Electronics. Each category currently contains 20 finds. Product cards pair an image and product name with a reference price, a direct destination link and quick access to category-appropriate quality-control ideas.

The spreadsheet is designed for product discovery. It does not replace the live listing. Before paying, check the selected size or model, seller information, current price, stock status and all terms visible on the destination page. Product pages can change after a record is added, so a link should be treated as a starting point for verification.

How to use a W2C list without skipping verification

  1. Open the closest category instead of searching the entire catalog at random.
  2. Compare more than the reference price. Confirm the current variant, size, color, quantity and included accessories.
  3. Save the exact product name and source link before ordering so you can match the warehouse record later.
  4. Use the QC Finder to turn the product type into a short photo and measurement checklist.
  5. Keep the order separate from shipping estimates until the item reaches the warehouse and actual package information is available.

Why the catalog uses six stable categories

Stable categories make the list easier to scan and give each product a predictable set of QC questions. Footwear benefits from shape and size measurements. Clothing needs flat measurements and seam checks. Bags need structure, hardware and interior photos. Electronics need exact model, contents and basic function checks. A consistent directory also makes it easier to return later and see what changed.

Reference price, live price and total cost are different

The amount shown on a product card is a discovery reference. The product price can change, and total buying cost can also include seller delivery, payment-related charges, optional warehouse services, international shipping and destination charges. Use the separate shipping calculator to model chargeable weight, then confirm live routes and terms in the relevant account before submitting a parcel.

LitBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is LitBuyVIP the official LitBuy website?

No. LitBuyVIP is an independent product-discovery and buyer-education resource.

Do all 120 products stay available?

No. Listings and stock can change. Always verify the destination page before buying.

What does W2C mean?

W2C commonly means “want to cop” and describes search intent for a usable product or purchase link.
