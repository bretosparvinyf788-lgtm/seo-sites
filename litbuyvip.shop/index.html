REVERSE PURCHASING KNOWLEDGE

Reverse purchasing questions FAQ

Ten common questions buyers ask when sourcing from Chinese marketplaces, reviewing QC, consolidating parcels and arranging international delivery.

01What is reverse purchasing?+
Reverse purchasing usually means an overseas buyer uses a China shopping agent to source from marketplaces such as Taobao, Weidian or 1688, then uses the agent for purchasing, warehousing, QC, consolidation and international shipping.
02How does a LitBuy Spreadsheet help?+
A spreadsheet organizes product names, categories, prices, images, item IDs, QC references and working links so buyers can compare options and verify details before ordering.
03Which Chinese marketplaces can be sourced?+
Common sources include Taobao, Weidian and 1688. Whether an item can be purchased or shipped depends on seller status, inventory, product attributes and the agent’s restrictions.
04Does LitBuyVIP place orders or collect payment?+
No. LitBuyVIP is an independent discovery and purchasing-research site. Ordering, payment, warehousing and shipping are completed through the linked main site or agent platform.
05Why do products open on the main site?+
Each product card connects to its corresponding main-site detail page so buyers can review fuller information and continue the purchasing process. Always recheck the name, option, price and link status.
06Are QC photos always free?+
Free standard photos, the included photo count and the cost of extra angles vary by platform and service. Check the fee displayed in the account before requesting measurements, extra photos or video.
07What costs are involved in reverse purchasing?+
The total may include product price, domestic China shipping, international freight, payment or exchange-rate costs, plus optional insurance, reinforcement, box removal, extra photos and storage.
08Why can final shipping cost differ from the estimate?+
Routes may charge by actual or volumetric weight and apply minimum weights, rounding, product restrictions or surcharges. Final packed dimensions and weight provide a more reliable estimate.
09What should I do when a product link is dead or sold out?+
Search by product name, item ID, image or category and check the spreadsheet update date. Do not pay based only on an old screenshot; confirm the current link, price and inventory first.
10What should be checked before submitting an international parcel?+
Confirm quantity, color, size and SKU; review QC and measurements; resolve visible issues; and verify restrictions, packaging, declaration, insurance, coupon terms and chargeable weight.
