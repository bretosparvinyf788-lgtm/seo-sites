How to Use a LitBuy Spreadsheet: Find Products and Buy Step by Step

Learn how to search a LitBuy spreadsheet, open the source listing, choose a variant, review warehouse QC and plan shipping before paying.

Updated: 2026-10-08 · English

What a LitBuy spreadsheet does

A LitBuy spreadsheet is a product research list. It helps you compare names, categories, reference prices, images and links before using a shopping agent. The spreadsheet is not an order confirmation, stock guarantee or proof of product authenticity. LitBuyVIP is an independent research site; our product links open the corresponding kakobuymake.com catalog page. That catalog page and the seller listing are separate from your LitBuy account.

The practical sequence is research → verify listing → place an eligible order in the agent account → inspect warehouse evidence → arrange shipping. Keeping these stages separate prevents a cheap reference price from being mistaken for a delivered price.

1. Search by category, name or catalog ID

Use the ten category shortcuts to browse shoes, T-shirts, fashion clothing, hoodies, trousers, belts, hats, bags, accessories and electronics. The homepage search covers the 12 references displayed here. For a larger selection, open the complete main catalog.

A search for 2844, for example, finds the Nike Hyperdunk reference in this local list. This number is a main-catalog record ID, not a Weidian seller item ID. You can also paste a kakobuymake.com product link containing aid=2844. A seller URL containing another kind of ID must be checked on its own source platform; do not assume that the numbers are interchangeable.

2. Open the product and verify the live listing

Open the exact product page rather than the catalog homepage. Check that the product name, photos and selected variation agree. Then review the underlying marketplace listing: seller, price, stock, size, color, quantity and domestic delivery. Prices in this spreadsheet are references and exclude international shipping.

If a link is unavailable, search the main catalog by product name or category. Do not substitute a visually similar listing without comparing the variation and seller. Save the original URL and the listing details you used for your decision.

3. Choose the variant before ordering

A listing with many styles does not describe one interchangeable product. A displayed starting price can apply only to the cheapest variant. Select the exact SKU and recheck the resulting price. For shoes, use the seller’s size chart and request an appropriate measurement if needed. For hoodies and trousers, compare garment measurements with an item you already own.

Before paying, record the product URL, selected SKU, size, color, quantity, quoted product price, domestic delivery and any service fee. Check whether the account accepts the item and whether an international route supports its attributes. A successful domestic order does not establish international shipping eligibility.

4. Review warehouse QC and resolve problems

When the item is recorded at the warehouse, compare QC photos with your saved order details. Check quantity, color, labels, visible condition and the specific dimensions that matter for fit. If a question remains, request a focused photo or measurement and check its fee before confirming.

Keep an incorrect or damaged item out of the international parcel while you check return eligibility. Warehouse storage and seller return deadlines are different. Save arrival dates and act on seller issues promptly. Photographs help with visible checks; they cannot establish authenticity or reveal every hidden defect.

5. Calculate the delivered cost

Add product cost, domestic delivery, applicable account or payment fees, international freight, selected packing services and destination charges that apply. Compare routes using the packed actual weight and dimensions. A light shoe box may be billed by volume. Rehearsal packing can be useful when dimensions are uncertain; check the account’s fee and timing.

For a worked budget, imagine two products costing 120 CNY and 180 CNY, with 15 CNY combined domestic delivery. Your known purchase subtotal is 315 CNY. International freight, optional services and destination charges remain separate unknowns until verified. This example is arithmetic, not a live LitBuy quotation.

Example: research a hoodie without guessing its size

Open the Polo Bear hoodie reference (catalog ID 2813), inspect the current source page and find the selected variant’s chart. Measure a hoodie that fits you: chest laid flat, body length and sleeve length. Compare using the same units and measuring method. Save the requested variant, then check the warehouse measurement for that variant rather than relying on the size label alone.

The purchase can proceed only when the live price, seller terms, fit and shipping eligibility are acceptable. If the chart or QC measurement is missing, request that evidence. The product photo on this site is a catalog illustration, not a new measurement or a warehouse QC sample.

Questions before you buy

Is the spreadsheet itself the official LitBuy service? No. Use the official account for purchase, payment and parcel instructions. Does the reference price include shipping? No; calculate the separate cost components. Can a catalog ID be pasted into every agent? No; verify the actual marketplace product URL.

Keep a short record for each purchase: source URL, chosen SKU, listing price, QC decision, seller deadline, accepted route and final parcel cost. That record makes buying decisions easier to review and problems easier to explain to support.
