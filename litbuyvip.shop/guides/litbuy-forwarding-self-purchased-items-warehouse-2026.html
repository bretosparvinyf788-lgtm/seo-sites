How to Forward Self-Purchased Items to the LitBuy Warehouse Without Losing Track

Use LitBuy forwarding without losing track of self-purchased items: copy the live warehouse address, register domestic tracking, verify storage and prepare shipping.

Updated: 2026-10-08 · English

Forwarding looks simple because the physical journey is short at first: buy from a Chinese marketplace, send the domestic package to LitBuy, and ship it internationally later. The difficult part is not distance. It is identity. The warehouse must be able to connect a carton arriving under a domestic tracking number with the correct user, seller order and expected contents.

LitBuy’s current public forwarding workflow reflects this. It tells users to copy the live warehouse recipient information, purchase from the seller with that address, submit the domestic logistics number, and wait until the item shows “Stored.” Only then does the workflow move to My Warehouse, where stored items can be selected for packing, an international address can be entered, and the related charges can be paid.

A safe forwarding process therefore needs an unbroken record. You should be able to move from the marketplace order to the carrier scan, from the carrier scan to the forwarding submission, and from that submission to the warehouse item without guessing.

Choose forwarding for the right order

Forwarding is useful when you can place and manage the domestic purchase yourself. Perhaps the marketplace checkout accepts your payment, you already communicate with the seller, or you bought through a channel that does not need an agent to place the order. In that situation, LitBuy receives and later forwards the goods rather than initiating the seller purchase for you.

That distinction changes the control points. With a purchasing order, the agent-side order record begins before the seller ships. With a self-purchased forwarding order, you create the first records outside LitBuy. You are responsible for preserving the exact listing, selected variant, quantity, price, seller messages and domestic order number. If that source record is vague, the warehouse cannot reconstruct what you intended to buy.

Do not use forwarding merely to bypass a risk warning. Before paying the seller, confirm that the item can be accepted at the warehouse and has a plausible international route to your country. A successful domestic delivery does not make a prohibited or route-incompatible item shippable overseas.

Copy the live warehouse address, not a memory

The forwarding page displays the warehouse recipient, contact number and shipping address for copying. Use the current information shown in your own account when you place the domestic order. Do not rely on an address saved months ago, a screenshot from another user, or a shortened version typed from memory.

Chinese seller checkouts may divide an address into province, city, district and street fields. Compare the completed order with LitBuy’s displayed block before payment. Check the recipient text and contact number character by character. If the page provides an identifying code or account-specific text, keep it exactly as displayed; it helps separate your carton from many other arrivals.

Create a package map before shipment

One marketplace order is not always one physical carton. Sellers can split colors or sizes, ship an out-of-stock item later, or use separate tracking numbers for products stored in different locations. The reverse can also happen: a seller may combine two orders into one package.

Build a small package map as soon as tracking appears. For each expected carton, record the seller, marketplace order number, item names, variants, quantities, domestic carrier and tracking number. If the seller changes the number, keep both the old and new values with a note explaining the replacement.

This map prevents a common intake error: marking an entire order complete after only the first carton arrives. It also gives you something concrete to compare against the warehouse record. “Black hoodie, size M, quantity one” is actionable. “My Taobao order” is not.

Register every domestic tracking number

LitBuy’s forwarding instructions require the logistics tracking number to be submitted. Enter it after the seller has actually shipped and the carrier number is valid. Copying an order number or a pre-shipment placeholder into the tracking field creates a broken handoff.

Check the carrier name and full number against the marketplace logistics page. If one order has two tracking numbers, submit and monitor both. If two orders share one carton, note the expected contents under the same shipment record where the interface allows, and preserve the seller’s consolidation message.

Use a three-key match: seller order, domestic tracking number and LitBuy warehouse record. Do not move to international shipping until all three describe the same physical goods.

Separate “delivered” from “stored”

A carrier’s delivered scan means the domestic parcel reached the delivery location or was accepted under the carrier’s process. It does not necessarily mean warehouse intake, identification and shelving are complete. LitBuy’s public workflow specifically says to proceed after the status shows “Stored.”

Give the warehouse record time to appear, but watch the evidence. If the carrier shows delivery and the LitBuy record remains unmatched, verify the address, recipient, delivery time, proof of delivery and tracking number before contacting support. A concise ticket should include one package, one tracking number and one evidence set. Mixing several missing cartons into a vague message makes reconciliation harder.

Inspect before you combine

A forwarded product can be physically present but still be wrong. Check the visible identity, color, size marking, quantity and obvious condition using the warehouse information available for that item. Compare it with the seller order, not with memory or a generic listing title.

If something is inconsistent, pause parcel submission. Because you placed the seller order yourself, keep the marketplace’s after-sales deadline in view and retain all seller communications. Ask LitBuy support what warehouse assistance is available for the specific stored item before assuming a return or exchange can be completed.

Do not remove packaging or combine the item irreversibly while a seller dispute is still possible. Original labels, bags, boxes and SKU stickers can connect the received product to the seller transaction. They may also matter when the marketplace asks for evidence.

Plan the international parcel as a second job

Once every forwarded item is correctly stored, the international stage begins. LitBuy directs users to My Warehouse to select items, submit them for packing, enter the shipping address and pay the related fees. Treat this as a separate decision from the domestic purchase.

Review which items belong together, their packaging needs and the live routes available for the destination. A fragile item may need reinforcement. A bulky retail box may increase dimensions. A battery, liquid or other sensitive category may narrow route choice. Use current route information and realistic parcel data rather than assuming the method used for a previous haul will still fit.

Before payment, verify the international recipient address, selected contents, quantity, packaging instructions, route and amount shown. Keep the final parcel number connected to the same package map. The audit trail should now extend from seller checkout all the way to international dispatch.

Use a ten-minute forwarding audit

  1. Open the live LitBuy forwarding page and copy the current warehouse details.
  2. Compare every address field with the completed marketplace order before paying.
  3. Save the listing, variant, quantity, seller order number and payment record.
  4. Create one expected-package row for every domestic tracking number.
  5. Submit the valid carrier and tracking number to the forwarding workflow.
  6. Monitor domestic delivery and wait for the LitBuy status to show Stored.
  7. Match the stored item and available warehouse evidence to the seller order.
  8. Resolve shortages, wrong variants or visible defects before packing.
  9. Select only verified items in My Warehouse and review route compatibility.
  10. Save the parcel contents, address, route, charges and international tracking record.

This routine takes less time than rebuilding the history of an unidentified carton after it reaches a busy warehouse.

Final takeaway

LitBuy forwarding is not just an address-copying shortcut. It is a handoff between a marketplace order you control and a warehouse record LitBuy must identify. The safest users make that handoff explicit with the live address, a valid domestic tracking number and a detailed package map.

Wait for Stored, verify the actual item, preserve any seller remedy, and only then build the international parcel. When seller order, tracking number and warehouse record agree, forwarding becomes a controlled workflow instead of a search for a box that arrived without enough context.

FAQ

What is LitBuy forwarding?

Forwarding is the workflow for goods you purchase yourself and send to the LitBuy warehouse. You copy the current warehouse recipient details, submit the domestic tracking number, wait for storage, and then prepare an international parcel.

Should I reuse an old screenshot of the LitBuy warehouse address?

No. Copy the recipient, contact number and address from the live forwarding page for the current account and order. A saved address can become outdated or omit account-specific identification.

When should I submit the domestic tracking number?

Submit it as soon as the seller provides a valid number, after checking that the carrier and number match the seller order. Early registration makes the inbound package easier to reconcile.

What if one seller order is shipped in two packages?

Treat each domestic package as a separate physical arrival. Record every tracking number and identify which items are expected in each carton instead of assuming one order number equals one package.

Does carrier delivery mean the item is already stored?

Not necessarily. Domestic delivery and warehouse storage are separate events. LitBuy’s public forwarding workflow tells users to wait until the status shows Stored before selecting the item in My Warehouse.

Can I submit an international parcel before the forwarding item is stored?

The forwarding workflow directs users to select items from My Warehouse after they show Stored. Wait for that status and verify the item record before packing and payment.

What evidence should I keep for a forwarded purchase?

Keep the seller order page, variant and quantity, payment record, seller messages, domestic carrier and tracking number, delivery scan, forwarding record, warehouse item record and available inspection images.

Who should resolve a wrong item sent by the seller?

Start from the seller order and warehouse evidence. Because you placed the marketplace order yourself, preserve the marketplace dispute window and ask LitBuy support what warehouse assistance is available before authorizing international shipment.

Can forwarded goods be combined with other stored items?

LitBuy’s public workflow allows stored items to be selected in My Warehouse for packing. Before combining, confirm route compatibility, item identity, packaging needs and the complete warehouse records for every piece.

What is the safest final check before paying international shipping?

Match the seller order, every domestic tracking number and every warehouse item record one final time. Then review the destination, route, parcel contents, packaging instructions and amount shown before payment.
