Kakobuy FAQ: W2C, QC, Shipping & Fees

These answers distinguish Kakobuy’s documented policies from estimates and third-party catalog information.

No. This is an independent discovery and education site connected to kakobuymake.com. Kakobuy itself handles purchasing, warehouse operations, inspection options, parcel submission and international delivery.

W2C means “where to cop”—in plain language, where to find or buy an item. A useful W2C entry should show the marketplace source, product preview, price context, last-check date and a path to Kakobuy.

Standard photos are included when the applicable quality-inspection option is selected, but Kakobuy’s public rules do not promise a fixed number of item photos. Personalized photo requests are listed at ¥2 per image. Parcel photographs are a different service: three are free when selected during parcel submission.

No. They help identify visible issues such as color, quantity, sizing labels, stitching and obvious exterior defects. They do not verify authenticity, hidden construction, electronic function, material composition or long-term durability.

Kakobuy’s service-and-fee documentation says basic agent purchasing has no service fee. Buyers still pay the item price, domestic shipping to the warehouse, international shipping, possible customs-related charges, payment processing and any optional paid services.

It depends on destination, shipping line, product restrictions and billable weight. Billable weight is commonly the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight. Different lines may divide parcel dimensions by 5000, 6000, 7000 or 8000.

No. It is advertised as a coupon bundle with combined face value. Individual coupons can have minimum-spend rules, limited validity and shipping-only conditions, so the headline amount should not be treated as unrestricted credit.

The production catalog is designed to show a last-checked date per entry and prioritize recently verified links. Marketplace listings can still change or disappear between checks, so the final product page should always be reviewed before payment.

Dimensions, product attributes, destination, line eligibility, fuel or seasonal adjustments and volume-weight divisors can all differ. A bulky 5 kg parcel may be billed at a much higher volumetric weight than a compact 5 kg parcel.

Confirm the source marketplace, variation, size information, recent availability, domestic shipping, return conditions and what visual evidence will be available. Do not rely on a product title or one promotional image alone.
