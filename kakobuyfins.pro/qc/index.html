What Kakobuy inspection does not prove

The published inspection rules focus on visible information: style, quantity, color, size, model and obvious exterior problems. The service does not test electronic functions, certify authenticity, confirm specialist performance or necessarily open sealed packaging. A useful QC page should say this clearly instead of using “verified” as a blanket guarantee.

Production data rule: every QC gallery will show the photo date, product variation, source item, inspection scope and a clear “visual review only” notice.
