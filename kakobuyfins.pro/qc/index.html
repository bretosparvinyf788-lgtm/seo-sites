What Kakobuy inspection does not prove

The published inspection rules focus on visible information: style, quantity, color, size, model and obvious exterior problems. The service does not test electronic functions, certify authenticity, confirm specialist performance or necessarily open sealed packaging. A useful QC page should say this clearly instead of using “verified” as a blanket guarantee.

Data rule: every product QC gallery keeps the source item, variation context and a clear “visual review only” notice.

How to Read Kakobuy QC Photos

Start by matching the photo set to the exact product, color and size in your order. Then separate broad confirmation from decision-critical detail. An overall front image may confirm color and general shape, while a close-up or measurement is needed for a label, seam, printed detail or physical dimension.

Review images promptly after the order reaches the warehouse. If a missing view could change your keep, exchange or return decision, describe one precise angle or measurement in a personalized request. Clear instructions such as “show the size label in focus” or “place a tape measure across the chest from seam to seam” produce more useful evidence than “check the quality.”

Kakobuy QC Checklist by Product Type

Clothing

Confirm color, labeled size, front and back design, embroidery or print placement, visible stains and physical measurements when seller sizing is uncertain.

Shoes

Compare the pair together, check panel alignment, toe shape, visible glue, sole edges, size markings and included accessories.

Bags and accessories

Check hardware color, closures, straps, included pieces, surface marks and overall shape. Ask for a scale reference when size is hard to judge.

What QC Can and Cannot Tell You

Warehouse QC is strongest for visible information: quantity, broad color, model, size label and obvious exterior problems. It is not a laboratory or professional authentication service. Photos cannot prove material composition, long-term durability, water resistance, hidden construction or electronic performance. Sealed goods may also limit what can be seen.

A trustworthy product page therefore identifies the source item and keeps the matching QC images near the product record. It should not use unrelated gallery images or turn a visual review into an authenticity claim. On this site, product and QC media are mapped from the corresponding kakobuymake.com page so buyers can see the relevant evidence immediately below the item.
