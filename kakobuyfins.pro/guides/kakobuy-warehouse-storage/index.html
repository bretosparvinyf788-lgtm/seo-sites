Kakobuy Warehouse and Storage Time Explained

Understand Kakobuy’s 100-day warehouse storage period, when it starts, how QC and return timing differ, and how to combine products before parcel submission.

September 24, 2026Warehouse and storageAbout 1536 words

The Kakobuy warehouse is the point between a Chinese marketplace order and an international parcel. Products from different sellers can arrive there on different days, receive visible quality-control checks and wait while you decide whether to keep, return, exchange or combine them. The warehouse is useful because it gives buyers time to consolidate, but that time has a documented limit.

Kakobuy’s public help information states that the normal free storage period for purchasing and transshipment goods is 100 days, starting when an order reaches the “in storage” status. A reminder is described around day 90. This guide explains what that clock means, how it interacts with QC and returns, and how to plan a parcel before the oldest item reaches its deadline.

When Kakobuy warehouse storage starts

The storage period does not begin when you paste a marketplace link, pay the product order or wait for the seller to dispatch. It begins when the product has arrived, been processed and the order status is recorded as in storage. Seller delivery is a separate stage, so two items purchased on the same day may receive different storage start dates.

Use the date associated with each stored item rather than relying on the purchase date. If a haul contains products from several sellers, make a simple list of their storage dates. The first item to enter storage becomes the planning deadline for the combined parcel.

What the 100-day period is designed for

Free storage gives buyers time to collect goods from different listings, inspect the arrivals and submit them together. Consolidation can reduce repeated base charges and make parcel planning more efficient. It also allows a later product to join an earlier order without forcing immediate international shipping after every warehouse arrival.

That flexibility should be used deliberately. Waiting can make sense when a known product is already on its way. It is less sensible to keep adding uncertain purchases while the oldest item approaches the end of storage. A longer wish list is not automatically a better haul, especially when volume weight, product restrictions or return deadlines work against consolidation.

Day 1: verify identity and status

When an item enters storage, confirm the goods order number, seller, product, selected variation and received quantity. Match the warehouse record to the original order. Similar names and images can make two products easy to confuse, especially when several orders contain the same category or color.

Check whether the record shows an inspection result or visible issue. A stored status means the warehouse has the item; it does not mean you have approved every detail. Keep the product identity, source link and photo set together so later parcel decisions are not based on an unlabeled image.

Review QC while options are still open

Kakobuy’s standard quality-control description focuses on visible information such as style, quantity, color, size, model and obvious exterior problems. Review those photos shortly after arrival. Compare the received variation with what you selected and identify any missing decision-critical view.

If you need a personalized photo, make the request precise. Kakobuy publicly lists personalized images at ¥2 each. A label close-up, named measurement or clear view of a reported defect is more useful than a broad request for “better quality.” The purpose is to answer a specific keep-or-return question before the item simply sits in storage.

Storage time is not the return window

The 100-day warehouse period should not be confused with a seller return or exchange period. Kakobuy’s public help results indicate that after-sales applications may need to be made while an order is in warehouse status within a much shorter period, and seller acceptance still matters. Domestic return freight and processing conditions can also apply.

Resolve visible problems immediately. Waiting until day 90 can leave plenty of storage time but no practical seller remedy. Use the goods order number, identify the selected variation and reference the relevant image. A clear factual request is easier to process than a late general complaint.

Create a warehouse inventory

For a small haul, the Kakobuy account may provide enough organization. For several orders, maintain a list with product name, goods order number, in-storage date, QC status, return deadline if known, estimated weight and intended parcel. This is not a replacement for the account record; it is a planning view.

Mark items as unresolved, ready to ship or excluded. Do not let a product with an unanswered defect move into the ready group. If an item belongs to another person, record that ownership too. Good inventory control prevents the wrong product from being left behind or added to a parcel by mistake.

Decide what should be combined

Combining products can reduce duplicate packaging and repeated starting charges, but one large parcel is not always cheaper or safer. Bulky products can raise volumetric weight. Restricted attributes can remove otherwise attractive shipping lines. Fragile goods may need protection that makes aggressive compression unsuitable.

Group items by destination, urgency, restrictions and packing compatibility. Clothing can often share a compact parcel, while a fragile structured item may need space. If splitting is being considered, compare both complete live quotes. Two parcels create two base charges, declarations and tracking records, so a simple per-kilogram comparison can be misleading.

Understand estimated and final weight

Product weights shown before packing are useful for a rough plan. Final shipping depends on the completed parcel. Carriers may charge the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight, with the volume divisor varying by line. Boxes, protective material and optional services also affect the final dimensions.

Use rehearsal or packing information when a close estimate matters. Removing unnecessary seller packaging can reduce volume, but retain protection for delicate goods. The cheapest-looking packing choice is not a saving if it increases the risk of damage.

Plan backward from the oldest item

Treat day 90 as an action point, not the first time you notice the order. Kakobuy’s documented reminder around that date leaves limited room for unresolved photos, customer-service questions, packing and payment. A safer personal schedule is to review the haul well before the reminder and reserve time for unexpected problems.

At each review, ask four questions: Is every item correctly identified? Are QC and after-sales decisions complete? Are any expected products still worth waiting for? Which parcel must be submitted before the oldest item’s deadline? These questions keep the plan tied to evidence rather than continued browsing.

What can happen after the storage period

Kakobuy’s public storage rule warns that goods remaining beyond the normal period may be treated as abandoned under the applicable terms. Do not assume that a reminder extends the storage clock or guarantees recovery after expiration. If a deadline is close and parcel submission is blocked, contact Kakobuy through the available service channel before the period ends.

Take screenshots or record the status and your inquiry. A support message is not a substitute for completing the parcel, but a clear timeline is useful when an account or technical issue needs investigation. Avoid waiting for the last day, when time zones and service hours can remove practical options.

Moving from warehouse to parcel

When the ready items are selected, review the destination address, packing requests, declaration, available routes, insurance choices and final cost. Product restrictions and the destination determine which lines are eligible. A generic shipping calculator provides a planning range; Kakobuy’s live checkout determines the actual options and amount.

If you want package photographs, select that service during parcel submission. Kakobuy’s public information says three photographs of the completed package are free when the option is chosen. They record the external parcel and should not be confused with the earlier product QC images.

Keep records after submission

Save the parcel number, included goods orders, selected line, declared information and payment record. The warehouse stage ends, but those records connect the international package back to the individual products. If the parcel arrives damaged or incomplete, the association between order, QC set, packing record and tracking history becomes important.

Kakobuy’s public help asks users to report an abnormal package within 72 hours after signing. Photograph visible damage before opening, keep the label and document the unboxing when there is a concern. Prompt evidence supports a clearer service request than a description made after packaging has been discarded.

A practical Kakobuy storage schedule

  1. At warehouse arrival, record the in-storage date and verify product identity.
  2. Within the first review, check QC photos and request only the missing views that matter.
  3. Handle return or exchange questions immediately; do not use the 100-day storage period as an after-sales deadline.
  4. Maintain a ready-to-ship list and watch the oldest stored item.
  5. Estimate actual and volumetric weight before choosing whether to combine or split.
  6. Review the haul well before the day-90 reminder.
  7. Submit and pay for the parcel with enough time to solve a packing or checkout problem.
  8. Save parcel records and document delivery condition.

The warehouse is most valuable when it creates an organized pause between buying and shipping. Use that pause to verify products, resolve problems and build a parcel around the oldest storage date. The published 100-day period is generous for consolidation, but it does not extend seller return rules and should never become a reason to postpone every decision until the final reminder.
